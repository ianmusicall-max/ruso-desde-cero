A1 · Unit 2 · Week 3 · Day 5 · Lesson

Asking with кто, что and а

Кто э́то? А э́то?

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you learn two new intonations, the one in Кто э́то? and the one in А вы?, and seven words for talking about people. By the end you'll be able to:

  • ask Кто э́то? with your voice leaning on кто;
  • ask А э́то? with your voice going up and staying up;
  • tell подру́га from де́вушка.

1Warm-up0–3 min

Read aloud, with no voice at the end:

хлеб — сад — снег — глаз — моро́з — Ивано́в

2Pronunciation3–8 min

You already have two intonations: the statement, which falls (ИК-1), and the yes/no question, which jumps up and drops (ИК-3). Today, two more:

Intonation When How
ИК-2 questions with кто, что, как… the voice leans hard on the question word and falls: Кто↘ э́то?
ИК-4 questions starting with а the voice rises on the stressed syllable and stays up: А э́↗то?

Слу́шайте и повторя́йте. Listen and repeat, watching where the voice falls and where it stays up.

ИК-2: Кто э́то? · Что э́то? · Как дела́?
ИК-4: А э́то? · А вы? · А ты?

ИК-2 sounds firm, almost like a friendly order: you don't need to raise your voice at the end to make it a question, because кто already does that. ИК-4 sounds open, as if you left the sentence hanging, waiting for the answer.

3New8–18 min

Seven words for the people around you:

Word Say it Means
друг drook friend (a man)
подру́га pa-DROO-ga friend (a woman)
сосе́д sa-SYET neighbour (a man)
сосе́дка sa-SYET-ka neighbour (a woman)
де́вушка DYE-voosh-ka girl, young woman
ма́льчик MAL'-chik boy
челове́к chi-la-VYEK person

— Кто э́то?
Who's that?
— Э́то сосе́д, Макси́м.
It's the neighbour, Maxim.
— А э́то?
And her?
— Э́то сосе́дка, Ни́на.
That's the neighbour, Nina.
— А э́то? Де́вушка?
And this one? A girl?
— Нет, э́то не де́вушка, а ма́льчик!
No, it's not a girl, it's a boy!

Look at сосе́д — сосе́дка: the woman gets -ка at the end. English usually doesn't mark it at all (neighbour for both). But друг — подру́га is an irregular pair: learn it as two words.

⚠️ Contrast with English

Your voice is already halfway there. Who's that? falls in English too, like ИК-2, and And you? rises, like ИК-4. What's new is in the words.

Подру́га isn't "girlfriend", and де́вушка can be. Подру́га is a female friend. Де́вушка is a young woman, and with "my" in front, a girlfriend in the romantic sense:

You mean Say
a (female) friend подру́га
a girl, a young woman де́вушка
my girlfriend (romantic) моя́ де́вушка ("my" comes in unit 7)

So when a woman says "Э́то моя́ подру́га", she's introducing a friend, the way English speakers say a girlfriend of mine about a mate. Mix them up and the sentence still works, but it tells a different story.

4Practice18–25 min

А. Чита́йте.

Read each question with its intonation: ИК-2 or ИК-4.

Кто э́то? — А э́то? — Что э́то? — А вы? — Как дела́? — А ты?

Б. Мужчи́на и́ли же́нщина?

Is it a man or a woman?

  1. сосе́дка
  2. друг
  3. подру́га
  4. ма́льчик
  5. де́вушка
  6. сосе́д

В. Кака́я интона́ция?

ИК-2 or ИК-4?

  1. Кто э́то?
  2. А э́то?
  3. Как дела́?
  4. А вы?

5Your turn25–29 min

Your album. Find three photos with people on your phone and introduce them, as if you were showing them to a Russian friend:

Кто э́то? — Э́то друг. А э́то? — Э́то подру́га. А э́то? — Э́то сосе́д.

Record yourself. Listen: does Кто э́то? fall firmly, and does А э́то? stay up?

6Wrap-up29–30 min

I can now say:
Кто э́то? · А э́то? · Э́то сосе́д. · Э́то не де́вушка, а ма́льчик!

I know: with кто, что and как the voice leans and falls (ИК-2); with а, it rises and stays (ИК-4).
