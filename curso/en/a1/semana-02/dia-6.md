> **Today** you check the whole of unit 1: reading, knowing the words and replying in the dialogues. By the end you'll know what's solid and which days to go back to before unit 2.

## 1. Instructions (0–2 min)

Fifteen questions in three blocks, each with four answers and only one
right. **Don't look at the lessons** and don't use a dictionary: if you don't
know something, skip it and move on. Note down the letter of each answer on
a piece of paper. You have 23 minutes, more than enough; if you finish
early, read block 1 aloud again.

## 2. Test (2–25 min)

### 1. Чте́ние

*How is it said?*

1. хорошо́ → а) [kha-ra-SHO] · б) [kho-ro-SHO] · в) [kha-RO-sho] · г) [kho-ro-MO]
2. спаси́бо → а) [SPA-si-boh] · б) [spa-SEE-ba] · в) [spa-SEE-boh] · г) [spa-si-BOH]
3. что → а) [chto] · б) [kto] · в) [sto] · г) [shto]
4. подъе́зд → а) [pod-YEZD] · б) [pad-YEST] · в) [po-DEZD] · г) [PA-dyest]
5. пожа́луйста → а) [pa-ZHAL-sta] · б) [po-zha-LOO-ee-sta] · в) [pa-zha-LOOY-sta] · г) [PO-zhal-sta]

### 2. Ле́ксика

*What does it mean?*

6. газе́та → а) magazine · б) film · в) newspaper · г) photo
7. пло́хо → а) well · б) too · в) badly · г) fine
8. извини́те → а) thank you · б) excuse me · в) you're welcome · г) goodbye
9. ёлка → а) cheese · б) yacht · в) humour · г) New Year tree
10. то́же → а) or · б) and · в) no · г) too

### 3. Диало́ги

*Choose the reply that fits.*

11. — Как дела́? — … → а) Меня́ зову́т Ки́ра. · б) Пожа́луйста. · в) Норма́льно, спаси́бо. А у тебя́? · г) До свида́ния.
12. — Спаси́бо! — … → а) Пожа́луйста. · б) Хорошо́. · в) Пока́! · г) Извини́те!
13. — Ко́фе и́ли чай? — … → а) Да. · б) Чай, пожа́луйста. · в) Нет. · г) Э́то чай?
14. You're saying goodbye to Vera Ivanovna, whom you call вы. → а) Пока́! · б) Приве́т! · в) Как дела́? · г) До свида́ния!
15. Which one is said with the voice jumping up on the stressed syllable (ИК-3)? → а) Кто там? · б) Там метро́. · в) Там метро́? · г) Что э́то?

## 3. Key and score (25–29 min)

Now open the answers at the bottom of the page and mark your test. One point
per right answer.

| Points | What it means |
|---|---|
| 13–15 | Unit 1 is done. On to unit 2. |
| 10–12 | Good, with gaps: go back to the days of your mistakes before moving on. |
| fewer than 10 | Redo week 2 before unit 2: everything that follows rests on it. |

Each answer in the key says which day teaches it. Read the explanation of
the traps too: the mistakes in this test are nearly always the same ones,
and they have names.

## 4. What to review (29–30 min)

Write down the days of your mistakes. If you slipped in block 1, review the
unstressed о rule first (week 1, day 3): half of Russian pronunciation rests
on it.

## Answers

**1. Чте́ние:** 1 а (wk 2, day 1) · 2 б (wk 2, day 1) · 3 г (wk 1, day 5) · 4 б
(wk 2, day 5) · 5 а (wk 2, day 2)

**2. Ле́ксика:** 6 в (wk 2, day 2) · 7 в (wk 2, day 1) · 8 б (wk 2, day 2) · 9 г
(wk 2, day 4) · 10 г (wk 2, day 1)

**3. Диало́ги:** 11 в (wk 2, day 5) · 12 а (wk 2, day 2) · 13 б (wk 2, day 3) ·
14 г (wk 2, day 5) · 15 в (wk 1, day 5)

**The traps.** In 1, two of the three о's in **хорошо́** are unstressed: they
sound [a]. In 2, the final о of **спаси́бо** is unstressed too: [ba], not
[boh]. In 3, **что** is an exception: [shto]. In 4, the **ъ** separates
([pad-YEST]) and the final д sounds almost like a t. In 5, the у and й of
**пожа́луйста** almost vanish. In 13, a question with **и́ли** isn't answered
with "да". In 15, ИК-3 is the yes/no question tune; with **кто** or **что**
the voice doesn't jump like that.
