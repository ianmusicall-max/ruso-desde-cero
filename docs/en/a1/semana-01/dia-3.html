A1 · Unit 1 · Week 1 · Day 3 · Lesson

В, Е, Н — more letters that lie

В, Е, Н — ещё обма́нщики

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you learn three more tricky letters and the golden rule of Russian pronunciation. By the end you'll be able to:

  • read В, Е, Н;
  • read метро́, рестора́н, раке́та and Москва́, and know why Москва́ sounds [mas-KVA];
  • say вот ("here's") and нет ("no").

1Warm-up0–3 min

Read yesterday's words aloud, quickly:

сок · торт · ка́рта · ма́ска · ко́смос · у́тро · у́хо · рука́

Did a С or a Р catch you out? Notice how the last о of у́тро and ко́смос sounded: today you find out why.

2Pronunciation3–8 min

Three more letters that look English and aren't:

Letter Looks like Sounds As in
В в B [v] van
Е е E [ye] yes; after a consonant it softens it: не ≈ "nye"
Н н H [n] no

Слу́шайте и повторя́йте. Listen and repeat:

ва — во — ву · на — но — ну
ве — не — ме — те — ре

Е after a consonant. In не the [n] and the [ye] melt into one sound, the way n and y do in canyon. Don't say "nuh-yeh" in two beats: it's one quick syllable.

3New8–18 min

With 12 letters you can read words you understand at a glance. Start with a classic: on a Russian street you'll see РЕСТОРА́Н, and your eye will read "PECTOPAH". Now you can read it:

Word Say it Means
нет nyet no
вот vot here's, there's (pointing)
метро́ mi-TRO metro, underground
рестора́н ri-sta-RAN restaurant
раке́та ra-KYE-ta rocket
Москва́ mas-KVA Moscow
окно́ ak-NO window
ко́мната KOM-na-ta room

One-syllable words (вот, нет, кот) carry no mark: the stress has nowhere else to go.

The golden rule: an unstressed о sounds [a]. Look at окно́: it has two о's, and only the stressed one sounds [o]; the other sounds [a]. The same in Москва́ [mas-KVA] and рестора́н [ri-sta-RAN]. An unstressed е weakens too, towards a short [i]: метро́ [mi-TRO]. Unit 2 deals with that; for now, just don't say it loud.

And two words for every day. Вот points at something you see or hand over; нет is "no":

— Вот метро́!
Here's the metro!

— Там рестора́н?
Is there a restaurant there?
— Нет, там метро́.
No, the metro's there.

The question uses the same words as the answer: only your voice turns it into a question. You'll practise that in lesson 5.

⚠️ Contrast with English

Familiar words, Russian stress. Russian took these words from other languages and put the stress where it pleased:

English Russian
metro метро́ [mi-TRO]
restaurant рестора́н [ri-sta-RAN]
rocket раке́та [ra-KYE-ta]

Say them with the English stress and a Russian has to think twice. Learn each word with its stress, as if the stress were one more letter.

The good news: you already reduce vowels. In banana only the middle a is a full vowel; the other two are weak. Russian does the same with о: the stressed one is a full [o], the others weaken to [a] — Москва́ [mas-KVA], окно́ [ak-NO]. The catch is knowing which syllable is stressed, and that's why this course marks it on every word.

4Practice18–25 min

А. Чита́йте.

Read aloud. Before each word, find the stressed о: all the others sound [a].

окно́ — ко́мната — Москва́ — метро́ — рестора́н — ко́смос — вот — нет — Анто́н — Ве́ра

Б. [о] и́ли [а]?

Say how the bold о sounds: [о] or [а].

  1. Москва́
  2. окно́
  3. ко́мната
  4. ко́смос
  5. Анто́н
  6. окно́

В. Соедини́те.

Match each word with its translation.

  1. окно́
  2. ко́мната
  3. раке́та
  4. нет
  5. вот

а) room · б) no · в) here's · г) rocket · д) window

5Your turn25–29 min

Tour guide. Imagine you're showing a Russian friend round your town, map in hand. Point and present, with вот and там:

Вот ка́рта. Вот метро́. А там — рестора́н.
Here's the map. Here's the metro. And over there, a restaurant.

— Там Москва́?
— Нет!

Then write В, Е, Н by hand, capital and small, and today's eight words. Circle every unstressed о: those sound [a].

6Wrap-up29–30 min

I can now say:
Вот метро́! · Нет, там рестора́н. · Москва́ [mas-KVA].

I know: А, О, К, М, Т, С, Р, У, Х, В, Е, Н — 12 of 33 letters.
