> **Today** Camila finally has furniture and celebrates the новосе́лье, the housewarming. By the end you'll have read a whole scene from unit 10 without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**In Russia, when someone moves into a new apartment, there's a
custom that has nothing to do with the furniture: it has to do with
the cat.** Before reading, think: which animal would you let into a
new home first, and why?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Эпизо́д 10. Новосе́лье

*Camila now has furniture. Today is the новосе́лье: Vera Ivánovna,
Tom, Kira, and Misha come to see the apartment.*

> **Ками́ла:** Вот моя́ кварти́ра!
> **Ве́ра Ива́новна:** А где Ба́рсик?
> **Ми́ша:** Вот он. Э́то кот!
> **Ве́ра Ива́новна:** Снача́ла кот.
> **Ками́ла:** Да, снача́ла Ба́рсик.

*Barsik goes in first, slowly, and looks over every room.*

> **Том:** Где мо́жно чай?
> **Ками́ла:** Чай на ку́хне. Том, вот моя́ ку́хня!
> **Ки́ра:** А гости́ная где?
> **Ками́ла:** Вот гости́ная.
> **Ве́ра Ива́новна:** А где твой люби́мый дива́н?
> **Ками́ла:** Он здесь.
> **Том:** Спаси́бо, Ками́ла!
> **Ками́ла:** Пожа́луйста!

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**Снача́ла** ("first," "before anything else") is the only word you
hadn't seen until today; the rest of the episode comes from units 1
through 10.

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| снача́ла | first | Снача́ла кот. |

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Barsik is the last one to enter the apartment.
2. The tea is in the kitchen.
3. Camila's favourite sofa is in the living room.

### Б. Why?

*Why does Vera Ivánovna insist that Barsik go in first, before the people?*

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. А где твой люби́мый дива́н?
2. Снача́ла кот, а пото́м мы.

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** the Russian custom of letting the cat into a new apartment first, and **снача́ла** for "first" or "before anything else."
>
> With this you close unit 10. Next week, unit 11.

## Answers

**А.** 1 false: he goes in first · 2 true · 3 true

**Б.** It's a Russian custom: people believe that a cat going in
first brings good luck to the new home.

**В.** 1 And where's your favourite sofa? · 2 First the cat, and
then us.
