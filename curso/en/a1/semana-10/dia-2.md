> **Today** you learn not to sit there silently while the other person talks — in Russian that's what keeps a conversation going. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - fire off six reactions without thinking;
> - say **Как интере́сно!** and not **Что интере́сно!**;
> - hear the difference between **Пра́вда?** and **Пра́вда.**

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's jobs, with **он** and with **она́**:

> Он спортсме́н. Она́ спортсме́нка. Он гид. Она́ гид.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Two of today's words have a catch:

| Word | Say it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| молоде́ц | ma-la-**DYETS** | both unstressed **о** sound [a] |
| серьёзно | sir-**YOZ**-na | **ё** always takes the stress, and **ь** softens the **р** |

**Ё** is the one Russian letter that needs no stress mark: wherever there's
an **ё**, that's where the stress is. That's why **серьёзно** is never
written with the little line.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> кла́ссно — здо́рово — молоде́ц
> серьёзно — пра́вда — интере́сно

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**A Russian conversation isn't listened to in silence.** The listener keeps
dropping short words in; if they don't, the speaker assumes there's no
interest and stops. These are the six most useful:

| Word | Say it | When |
|---|---|---|
| кла́ссно | **KLAS**-na | great!, cool! (among friends) |
| здо́рово | **ZDO**-ra-va | brilliant! |
| молоде́ц | ma-la-**DYETS** | well done!, good for you! (to a person) |
| серьёзно | sir-**YOZ**-na | seriously? |
| Как интере́сно! | kak in-ti-**RYES**-na | how interesting! |
| Что ты! | shto ty | you don't say!, come off it! |

**Молоде́ц** is for the person, not the thing: you say it to someone who has
done something well, like "good on you". And **Что ты!** goes to someone you
call **ты**; the polite form is **Что вы!**

**And one you already knew**, from unit 2, which now doubles as a reaction:
**Пра́вда?** ("really?"). The only thing that changes is your voice, as in
unit 1: up on the stressed syllable, down right after.

> — Я рабо́таю. Я врач.
> *I work. I'm a doctor.*
> — Пра́вда? Как интере́сно!
> *Really? How interesting!*
> — А ещё я худо́жница.
> *And I'm a painter too.*
> — Серьёзно? Молоде́ц!
> *Seriously? Good for you!*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**Here English helps you more than usual.** *How interesting!* maps straight
onto **Как интере́сно!** — the word for "how" is the same in both the
question and the exclamation. The thing to avoid is the literal *what*:

| You mean | ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|---|
| How interesting! | ~~Что интере́сно!~~ | Как интере́сно! |
| How nice! | ~~Что хорошо́!~~ | Как хорошо́! |

**What English doesn't prepare you for is the silence.** An English
listener can get by with a nod and an occasional *mm*. In Russian, a pause
with nothing in it reads as "I'm not interested". Say something — even one
word.

**And watch the tune of «Пра́вда?»** English lifts the voice at the *end* of
*really?*; Russian jumps up on the stressed syllable, the first one of
**пра́вда**, and drops straight after. Rising at the end is understood, and it sounds
foreign.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, each with its own tune: the exclamations fall at the end, and
**Пра́вда?** and **Серьёзно?** jump up on the strong syllable.*

> Кла́ссно! · Здо́рово! · Молоде́ц! · Как интере́сно!
> Пра́вда? · Серьёзно? · Что ты!

### Б. Что сказа́ть?

*What would you say? Choose.*

1. A friend tells you they speak five languages. → а) Пожа́луйста. · б) Как интере́сно!
2. Your eighty-year-old neighbour says she's an athlete. → а) Серьёзно? · б) Извини́те.
3. A child shows you a drawing of his. → а) Молоде́ц! · б) Пра́вда.
4. Someone tells you something you find impossible. → а) Что ты! · б) Спаси́бо.

### В. Как и́ли что?

*Fill in.*

1. ______ интере́сно!
2. ______ э́то? — Э́то визи́тка.
3. ______ хорошо́!
4. ______ ты де́лаешь?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Listen and react.** Read these four sentences aloud and answer each one
with a different reaction, never repeating:

> Я рабо́таю в метро́. · Я говорю́ по-кита́йски. · Я не рабо́таю.
> Моя́ специа́льность — врач.

Then **record yourself** saying the six reactions one after another and
listen back: does your voice go flat? A reaction said without a tune doesn't
sound like a reaction — it sounds like a list.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> Кла́ссно! · Здо́рово! · Молоде́ц! · Серьёзно? · Как интере́сно! · Что ты!
>
> **I know:** **как** for exclaiming, **что** for asking. And in Russian, the listener talks too.
