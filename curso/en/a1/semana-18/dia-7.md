> **Today** is Sunday, the day off, and everyone in the courtyard does their own thing. By the end you'll have read a whole scene from unit 9 without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**A day off doesn't mean everyone does the same thing: it means
everyone chooses.** Today you'll see how four people spend the same
Sunday in four different ways, with none of them more correct than
another.

Before reading, think: of this unit's hobbies —гита́ра, гото́вить,
ша́хматы—, which would be your Sunday?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Эпизо́д 9. Выходно́й

*Sunday morning, in the courtyard. Tom is playing the guitar, Camila
is cooking, and Misha is playing chess with Vera Ivánovna.*

> **Том:** Сего́дня выходно́й. Я игра́ю на гита́ре.
> **Ками́ла:** А я гото́влю. Я рисова́ю.

*Misha laughs.*

> **Ми́ша:** Не «рисова́ю», а «рису́ю»! Ова́ исчеза́ет.
> **Ками́ла:** А, да. Я рису́ю.
> **Ве́ра Ива́новна:** Ми́ша, ты игра́ешь в ша́хматы хорошо́.
> **Ми́ша:** Спаси́бо! Мне нра́вятся ша́хматы.
> **Том:** Мне то́же нра́вится ша́хматы.

*Misha laughs again.*

> **Ми́ша:** Не «нра́вится», а «нра́вятся»! Ша́хматы — мно́го.
> **Том:** А, поня́тно. Мне нра́вятся ша́хматы.

*Kira comes out the door, still in her pajamas.*

> **Ки́ра:** До́брое у́тро! Я спа́ла мно́го. Сего́дня выходно́й!

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**There isn't a single new word today**: the whole episode comes
from units 1 through 9. **Спа́ла** is the past-tense form of "to
sleep"; the full past tense comes later, but here it's understood
from context.

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Я спа́ла мно́го. | I slept a lot. | Я спа́ла мно́го. |

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Tom is cooking.
2. Misha plays chess well.
3. Kira got up early.

### Б. Find the mistake.

*Find the mistake: Camila and Tom each make one slip. What is each one, and who corrects it?*

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Мне нра́вятся ша́хматы.
2. Я спа́ла мно́го.

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** that Camila mixes up "рисова́ю" with "рису́ю" —expecting the root to stay the same, as in English— and that Tom forgets that **нра́вится/нра́вятся** agrees with WHAT is liked, not with who says it. Both mistakes are exactly what this unit warned you about.
>
> With this you close unit 9. Next week, unit 10.

## Answers

**А.** 1 false: Tom is playing the guitar · 2 true · 3 false: she
slept a lot, she got up late

**Б.** Camila says "Я рисова́ю" instead of "Я рису́ю" (Misha corrects
her). Tom says "Мне нра́вится ша́хматы" instead of "Мне нра́вятся
ша́хматы" (Misha corrects him too).

**В.** 1 I like chess. · 2 I slept a lot.
