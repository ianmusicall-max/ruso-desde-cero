> **Today** you meet the few Russian words that aren't read the way they're written: **что**, **коне́чно**, **сего́дня**, **со́лнце**… With them, you can read any word. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read the three traps right: the г that sounds [v], the ч that sounds [sh] and the letter that isn't said;
> - say **Здра́вствуйте** with a single в;
> - name six new words, from **сего́дня** to **ле́стница**.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Answer aloud with a short answer, whichever comes to you:

> Чай? · Там интерне́т? · Э́то пра́вда? · Ко́фе?

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Almost all of Russian is read with the unit's rules. What's left are three
traps, and each one hides in just a few words:

**1. The г that sounds [v].** In a few words ending in `-ого` or `-его`,
like **его́** and **ничего́**, and in **сего́дня**. Later you'll see it in
adjective endings. But not in all of them: **немно́го** ("a little"),
which comes in unit 3, is said with г.

> его́ [yi-VO] · ничего́ [ni-chi-VO] · сего́дня [si-VOD-nya]

**2. The ч that sounds [sh].** In **что** and in a few words with **чн**:

> что [shto] · коне́чно [ka-NYESH-na] · ску́чно [SKOOSH-na]

Not in all of them: **то́чно** keeps its ч, [TOCH-na]. That's why these are
learnt one by one.

**3. The letter that isn't said.** When lots of consonants pile up, one gets
lost:

> здра́вствуйте [ZDRAST-vooy-tye] · со́лнце [SON-tse] · пра́здник [PRAZ-nik] · ле́стница [LYES-ni-tsa]

In **здра́вствуйте** the first в isn't said; in **со́лнце**, the л; in
**пра́здник**, the д; in **ле́стница**, the т.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

Six words with a trap:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| сего́дня | si-**VOD**-nya | today |
| со́лнце | **SON**-tse | sun |
| ску́чно | **SKOOSH**-na | boring; I'm bored |
| пра́здник | **PRAZ**-nik | holiday, celebration |
| ле́стница | **LYES**-ni-tsa | stairs, staircase |
| его́ | yi-**VO** | him, his (coming soon) |

And an old friend: **Ничего́!**, "no problem!", from week 2, has the г that
sounds [v] too. And it's also an answer to **Как дела́?**: "not bad".

> — Приве́т! Как дела́?
> *Hi! How are things?*
> — Ничего́. А у тебя́?
> *Not bad. And you?*
> — Ску́чно…
> *I'm bored…*
> — Сего́дня пра́здник!
> *Today's a holiday!*
> — Пра́вда? Здо́рово!
> *Really? Great!*

**Ску́чно** on its own is already a sentence: "how boring". To say "I'm
bored", use the **мне** from the café: **Мне ску́чно.**

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**You're used to silent letters, just not in Russian.** English is full of
them: the k in "knife", the d in "Wednesday", the s in "island". The trap is
the opposite: having heard that Russian "is read as it's written", you say
every letter:

| Word | ✗ Letter by letter | ✓ It's said |
|---|---|---|
| сего́дня | ~~[si-GOD-nya]~~ | [si-VOD-nya] |
| что | ~~[chto]~~ | [shto] |
| со́лнце | ~~[SOLN-tse]~~ | [SON-tse] |
| здра́вствуйте | ~~[ZDRAV-stvooy-tye]~~ | [ZDRAST-vooy-tye] |

The first в of **здра́вствуйте** is your d in "Wednesday": written, never
said.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, with the three traps.*

> что · коне́чно · ску́чно · его́ · ничего́ · сего́дня · здра́вствуйте · со́лнце · пра́здник · ле́стница

### Б. Кака́я бу́ква?

*Which letter isn't said, or sounds like something else?*

1. со́лнце
2. здра́вствуйте
3. сего́дня
4. ску́чно
5. пра́здник
6. ле́стница

### В. Отве́тьте.

*Answer with a phrase from today.*

1. — Как дела́? (you can't complain)
2. — Как дела́? (you're bored)
3. — Сего́дня пра́здник? (probably)
4. — Э́то со́лнце? (of course it is)

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your day, in four sentences.** Say what today is like and record yourself:

> Сего́дня… (a holiday? sun?) · Как дела́? — … · Мне ску́чно? И́ли нет?

Listen for the traps: does **сего́дня** come out with [v]? Does the л of
**со́лнце** sneak in? Then write the six new words by hand and cross out the
letter that isn't said.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> сего́дня, со́лнце, ску́чно, пра́здник, ле́стница, его́ · Ничего́. · Мне ску́чно. · Сего́дня пра́здник!
>
> **I know:** in **его́**, **ничего́** and **сего́дня** the г sounds [v]; **что** and **коне́чно** go with [sh]; and in a cluster of consonants, sometimes one isn't said.
