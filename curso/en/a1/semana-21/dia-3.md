> **Today there are no new verbs: there's a third thing стои́т can describe.** By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - know that **стои́т** also works for an object with a base;
> - choose between **сиди́т/стои́т**, **лежи́т**, and **виси́т** for any object;
> - describe a whole floor plan combining all three.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

This week's four verbs, from memory:

> сиди́т · стои́т · лежи́т · виси́т

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Nothing new to read today: review the stress of **стои́т** and
**лежи́т**, always at the end.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> ла́мпа стои́т — ру́чка лежи́т — карти́на виси́т

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Стои́т isn't just for people: an object WITH A BASE, like a lamp,
is also "standing."** It's the same logic you already know: picture
the position before choosing the verb.

| Object | Position | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| ла́мпа | has a base, stands upright | **стои́т** |
| ру́чка | lying on the table | **лежи́т** |
| карти́на | hanging | **виси́т** |
| стул | has a base, stands upright | **стои́т** |

> Ла́мпа стои́т на столе́.
> *The lamp is on the table.*
> Ру́чка лежи́т на столе́.
> *The pen is on the table.*
> Стул стои́т в углу́.
> *The chair is in the corner.*
> Ключи́ лежа́т на полу́.
> *The keys are on the floor.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English describes a lamp and a pen the same way, with "is";
Russian tells them apart by shape: what has a base and stands
upright uses стои́т, what doesn't uses лежи́т.**

| English (no distinction) | Russian (by the object's shape) |
|---|---|
| The lamp IS on the table. | Ла́мпа **стои́т** на столе́. |
| The pen IS on the table. | Ру́чка **лежи́т** на столе́. |

The same place, "на столе́," with two different verbs: what
changes is the object's shape, not the place.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Ла́мпа стои́т на столе́. Ру́чка лежи́т на столе́. Стул стои́т в
> углу́.

### Б. Сиди́т/стои́т, лежи́т и́ли виси́т?

*Choose the correct verb.*

1. Ла́мпа ______ на по́лке.
2. Карти́на ______ на стене́.
3. Том ______ в коридо́ре.
4. Ру́чка ______ на дива́не.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Стул стои́т в углу́.
2. Ключи́ лежа́т на полу́.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**A plan of your table.** Describe four objects in front of you out
loud, choosing between сиди́т/стои́т, лежи́т, and виси́т.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** **стои́т** also describes objects with a base, like a lamp or a chair, and all three posture verbs combine with any place I already know.
