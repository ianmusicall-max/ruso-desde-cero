> **Today there are no new words: there's a list.** You read a simple programme —with numbers instead of times, to practice without extra complication— and talk about what it's advertising. By the end you'll have read a full unit-8 scene without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**A programme is a list of options, not a sentence.** What makes it
readable isn't grammar: it's knowing which number goes with which word,
and that's learned by looking at it, not by translating word for word.

Before you read, think: of this week's three hobbies —кино́, футбо́л,
бале́т— which would you pick first?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Saturday's programme

> Оди́н — кино́.
> Два — футбо́л.
> Три — бале́т.

*Camila and Tom look at the programme.*

> — Ты лю́бишь кино́?
> — Да, люблю́. А ты?
> — Я люблю́ бале́т.
> — А футбо́л?
> — Нет, я не люблю́ футбо́л.

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**No new words today**: the three hobbies and three numbers come from
unit 8 and unit 6. This is the one that makes the scene work, and you
already have it:

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ты лю́бишь кино́? | Do you like the movies? | Ты лю́бишь кино́? |

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Number 1 is football.
2. Camila likes ballet.
3. Tom likes football.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ты лю́бишь кино́?
2. Я не люблю́ футбо́л.

### В. Your own programme

*Write three numbers with three hobbies from the unit (кино́, футбо́л,
бале́т, джаз, рок) and ask someone out loud which one they like.*

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** how to read a list with a number in front of each option, and that questions with **лю́бишь** are answered with **Да, люблю́** or **Нет, не люблю́**, without repeating the noun if it's already clear.

Tomorrow: the verb **де́лать**, again.

## Answers

**А.** 1 false: 1 is the movies · 2 true · 3 false: Tom doesn't like
football

**Б.** 1 Do you like the movies? · 2 I don't like football.
