> **Today** you learn four tricky letters: they look English and sound different. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read **С, Р, У, Х** without your eye fooling you;
> - read **сок**, **торт**, **ка́рта**, **ма́ска**, **ко́смос**, **у́тро**, **у́хо** and **рука́**;
> - say what's in a place: **Там торт.**

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Read yesterday's words aloud and say what each one means. If you're not
sure, they're in yesterday's vocabulary:

> ма́ма · кот · кто · там · так · как

> — Кто там?
> — Ма́ма.

If one of them didn't come easily, read it twice more. Yesterday you had 5
letters; today you get to 9.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

These four letters **look** like English letters. They aren't:

| Letter | Looks like | Sounds | As in |
|---|---|---|---|
| С с | c | [s] | **s**it — never [k] |
| Р р | p | [r] | rolled, as in Spanish or Italian |
| У у | y | [u] | f**oo**d (short, no glide) |
| Х х | x | [x] | Scottish lo**ch**, German Ba**ch** |

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> са — со — су · ра — ро — ру · ха — хо — ху
> ас — ос — ус · ар — ор — ур · ах — ох — ух

**The Russian р is not the English r.** For the English *r* the tongue
doesn't touch anything; for **р** the tip taps just behind the top teeth.
Start from American *butter* or *gotta*: the quick *tt* in the middle is
already a tap. Say *gotta* fast, then **ра — ро — ру** with the same tap.
With practice the tap turns into a short roll.

**Х is not an h.** An English *h* is just breath; **х** is friction at the
back of the mouth, the sound you make clearing your throat very gently.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

You now have 9 letters, and with them a batch of words. You'll understand
several without a translation:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| сок | sok | juice |
| торт | tort | cake |
| ка́рта | **KAR**-ta | map; card |
| ма́ска | **MAS**-ka | mask |
| ко́смос | **KOS**-mas | space, cosmos |
| у́тро | **OO**-tra | morning |
| у́хо | **OO**-kha | ear |
| рука́ | roo-**KA** | hand, arm |

Notice that **у́тро** is said [**oo**-tra], not [oo-tro]: the final **о** has
no stress, so it sounds almost like an [a]. The same goes for the second
**о** in **ко́смос**. It's the most important rule of Russian
pronunciation, and tomorrow you get all of it.

With yesterday's **там** you can already say what's in a place:

> **Там торт.** — *There's a cake there.*
> **Там сок.** — *There's juice there.*
> **Там ка́рта.** — *There's a map there.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**Your eye reads English, and today it will fool you four times.** It sees
**С** and reads [k], sees **Р** and reads [p], sees **У** and reads [y],
sees **Х** and reads [ks]:

| Word | Your eye reads | It's said |
|---|---|---|
| сок | ~~cok~~ | [sok] |
| рот | ~~pot~~ | [rot] |
| у́хо | ~~yxo~~ | [**oo**-kha] |

The trick: for a few days, before you read a word, look for these four
letters and "translate" them in your head. After a week you won't need to.

**And keep the vowel pure.** English turns *oo* into a glide (*food*,
*you*). Russian **у** is short and steady: round your lips and hold them
still until the sound ends.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud. Watch out for today's four letters.*

> сок — рок — торт — рот — у́хо — у́тро — ко́смос — ма́ска — му́ка — мука́

*Did you spot **му́ка** and **мука́**? Same letters, two words: **му́ка** means "torment" and **мука́** means "flour". English does the same with the stress of **re**cord (a noun) and re**cord** (a verb), but in Russian it happens far more often, and normal texts don't mark it. That's why this course marks the stress every time.*

### Б. Соедини́те.

*Match each word with its translation.*

1. торт
2. у́хо
3. ка́рта
4. сок
5. рука́

а) juice · б) hand · в) cake · г) map · д) ear

### В. Что там?

*What's there? Look at the English word and write the sentence in Russian.*

1. (cake) → Там …
2. (map) → Там …
3. (mask) → Там …

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Point and name.** Point at things in your room or your kitchen and make
up what's there, with today's and yesterday's words:

> Там торт. Там сок. Там кот! Там ма́ма.

Then write **С, Р, У, Х** by hand — capital and small — three times each,
and below them today's eight words.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> Там торт. · Там сок. · Там ка́рта. · Как рука́?
>
> **I know:** А, О, К, М, Т, С, Р, У, Х — 9 of 33 letters.

pronunciation.

only in their stress.
