> **Today** you complete the possessive chart with "our." By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **наш/на́ша/на́ше/на́ши** for something that belongs to your group;
> - use **ваш/ва́ша/ва́ше/ва́ши** again, this time for the other group;
> - tell the two series apart by WHO is speaking, not who you're talking to.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's eight forms, out loud:

> твой — твоя́ — твоё — твои́ · ваш — ва́ша — ва́ше — ва́ши

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Наш** and **ваш** rhyme: they end the same way. The only difference is
the first letter.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> наш — на́ша — на́ше — на́ши
> наш стол — ваш стол

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Yesterday ваш was for one formal person; today it also covers a whole
group**, the one that isn't yours. And **наш** shows up, for your own.

| Word | Used with |
|---|---|
| наш / на́ша / на́ше / на́ши | something from YOUR group |
| ваш / ва́ша / ва́ше / ва́ши | something from THE OTHER group |

**The difference isn't ты or вы: it's who's talking.** If Kira and Misha
say "наш стол," it's their table, the two of them. If Camila tells them
"ваш стол," she means the table that belongs to them, even while
addressing each one as ты.

> — Э́то наш стол? А и́ли ваш?
> *Is this our table? Or yours?*
> — Ва́ша. На́ша там.
> *Yours. Ours is over there.*
> — А э́то на́ши ключи́?
> *And are these our keys?*
> — Нет, э́то ва́ши.
> *No, those are yours.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English keeps "your" for both one person and a group; Russian always
uses ваш for the other group, no matter who's in it.** What DOES change
in Russian is the noun's gender, not who you're speaking to:

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| our table | на́ша ла́мпа *(feminine)* |
| your keys | ва́ши ключи́ *(plural)* |

Once you've decided whose it is —your group or the other one— the
ending is decided, as always, by the noun.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> наш стол · ва́ша ру́чка · на́ше письмо́ · ва́ши ключи́

### Б. Наш и́ли ваш?

*Kira is talking about something that belongs to her family (her and
Misha). Choose the series.*

1. ______ кварти́ра. → а) На́ша · б) Ва́ша
2. ______ стол. → а) Наш · б) Ваш
3. ______ ключи́. → а) На́ши · б) Ва́ши

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Э́то наш стол.
2. Ва́ши ключи́ здесь.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Two groups.** Picture two families comparing their things: the table,
the keys, the flat. Say out loud what belongs to which group, using
**наш** and **ваш**:

> На́ша кварти́ра здесь. Ва́ша там.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Э́то наш стол. · Ва́ши ключи́ здесь. · На́ша кварти́ра.
>
> **Now I know:** the eight forms that were missing, and that **наш/ваш** is chosen by who's speaking, not who you're speaking to.

Tomorrow: **его́, её, их** — his, her, their.
