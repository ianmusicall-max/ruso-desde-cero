A1 · Unit 2 · Week 3 · Day 1 · Lesson

Not this, but that: не…, а…

Не…, а…

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you learn to say what something isn't, and what it is: Э́то не сок, а вода́. By the end you'll be able to:

  • negate a word with не, and keep it apart from нет;
  • correct yourself with а: "not this, but that";
  • name eight everyday objects.

1Warm-up0–3 min

Read last week's dialogue aloud, with your voice jumping in the question and falling in the answer:

— Э́то дом?
— Нет, э́то рестора́н.

Today you learn a shorter, more Russian way to say the same thing.

2Pronunciation3–8 min

The rhythm of a word. A Russian word is one strong syllable and a few weak ones, and the strong one is the boss. Read these words tapping your hand on the stressed syllable:

● ○ ○ ● ○ ○ ●
ру́чка Москва́ телефо́н
кни́га метро́ рестора́н
ла́мпа окно́ шокола́д

Слу́шайте и повторя́йте. Listen and repeat, with the beat in its place:

ру́чка — кни́га — су́мка — ла́мпа — ша́пка
ключ — зонт — телефо́н

Notice the final а of ру́чка or ла́мпа? It's unstressed: short and lazy, like the a at the end of sofa. And in кни́га you say both first consonants, к and н: [kni-ga]. The к isn't silent, as it is in knife.

3New8–18 min

Eight things you carry around or have close by:

Word Say it Means
ру́чка ROOCH-ka pen
кни́га KNEE-ga book
су́мка SOOM-ka bag
ключ klyooch key
зонт zont umbrella
телефо́н ti-li-FON phone
ла́мпа LAM-pa lamp
ша́пка SHAP-ka hat (a warm one)

Now the new part. To negate a word, put не right in front of it:

Э́то не кни́га.
It's not a book.

And to say what it is, а:

Э́то не кни́га, а журна́л.
It's not a book, it's a magazine.
Э́то не ко́фе, а чай.
It's not coffee, it's tea.

After a question, нет answers and не negates:

— Э́то ключ?
Is it a key?
— Нет, э́то не ключ, а ру́чка.
No, it's not a key, it's a pen.

Both halves fall (ИК-1): first on what it isn't, then on what it is.

⚠️ Contrast with English

Good news first: English splits "no" too. Нет is no (the answer) and не is not (in front of a word). Just drop is and a: It's not a book → Э́то не кни́га.

The trap is а: it isn't "and". Russian has two words where English uses and: и joins things (Anton and Anna), а contrasts them (not this, but that). In a correction, it's always а:

You mean ✗ Don't say ✓ Say
It's not a book, it's a magazine. Э́то не кни́га, и журна́л. Э́то не кни́га, а журна́л.
No, it's not a key. Не, э́то не ключ. Нет, э́то не ключ.

4Practice18–25 min

А. Чита́йте.

Read aloud, with the beat on the stressed syllable.

ру́чка — кни́га — су́мка — ла́мпа — ша́пка — ключ — зонт — телефо́н

Б. Отве́тьте по образцу́.

Answer as in the model. In brackets, what it really is.

Э́то кни́га? (журна́л) → Нет, э́то не кни́га, а журна́л.

  1. Э́то ру́чка? (ключ)
  2. Э́то зонт? (ла́мпа)
  3. Э́то сок? (вода́)
  4. Э́то ша́пка? (су́мка)
  5. Э́то телефо́н? (фо́то)

В. Не и́ли нет?

Fill in не or нет.

  1. — Э́то чай? — …, э́то ко́фе.
  2. Э́то … ру́чка, а ключ.
  3. — Э́то кни́га? — Нет, э́то … кни́га.
  4. — Там метро́? — …

5Your turn25–29 min

The "what it isn't" game. Point at things around you and get their names wrong on purpose; then correct yourself aloud:

Э́то ша́пка? Нет! Э́то не ша́пка, а су́мка.
Э́то не телефо́н, а ла́мпа!

Record five sentences like that. Listen: does your voice fall in both halves? Do the к and н of кни́га come together, with no vowel between them?

6Wrap-up29–30 min

I can now say:
Э́то не кни́га, а журна́л. · Нет, э́то не ключ. · ру́чка, су́мка, зонт…

I know: нет answers, не negates, а corrects.
