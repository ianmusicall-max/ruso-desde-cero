> **Today** you learn the two signs that make no sound and finish the alphabet. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read **Ь** and **Ъ**, and all 33 letters;
> - say goodbye the right way: **Пока́!**, **До свида́ния!**, **До встре́чи!**;
> - answer **Норма́льно** to a **Как дела́?**

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Read aloud. Exaggerate the ы:

> мы — ми · был — бил · Я Анто́н. Ты А́нна. Мы тури́сты.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Two letters with no sound of their own that change the letter next to them:

| Letter | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ь ь | soft sign | softens the consonant in front |
| Ъ ъ | hard sign | separates the consonant from the vowel after it |

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> брат — брать · у́гол — у́голь · день · фильм
> сел — съел · подъе́зд

**Ь softens.** English has no soft consonants at the end of a word, so this
takes practice. Say *canyon* and stop in the middle, on the *ny*: that's the
end of **день** [dyen']. Same with л: in **фильм** the l is soft, with the
middle of the tongue pressed against the roof of the mouth.

**Ъ separates.** In **сел** (sat down) the с melts into the е; in **съел**
(ate up) the с stays hard and then you hear a *y*: [s-yel]. Few words have
ъ, but you'll see one on every building: **подъе́зд**, the entrance.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

You know all 33 letters. What's left is knowing how to leave:

| Phrase | Say it | When |
|---|---|---|
| Пока́! | pa-**KA** | bye!: to people you call ты |
| До встре́чи! | da **FSTRYE**-chi | see you!: to anyone |
| До свида́ния! | da-svi-**DA**-ni-ya | goodbye!: to people you call вы |
| Споко́йной но́чи! | spa-**KOY**-nay **NO**-chi | good night! (going to bed) |

Notice the в in **встре́чи** sounding like f? Before с it rubs off: it's the
unit 4 rule you already met in **футбо́л**.

And a new answer to **Как дела́?**, the most Russian of all:

> — Как дела́?
> *How are things?*
> — Норма́льно.
> *Fine, OK. (Neither good nor bad: business as usual.)*

And three words with **ь** and **ъ**:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| день | dyen' | day |
| фильм | fil'm | film, movie |
| подъе́зд | pad-**YEST** | entrance (of a block of flats) |

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**Ь changes words, and your ear doesn't hear it.** English has no soft
consonants, so it's tempting to ignore the ь. But:

| Without ь | With ь |
|---|---|
| **брат** — brother | **брать** — to take |
| **у́гол** — corner | **у́голь** — coal |

**And *bye* isn't always Пока́.** In English *bye* works with almost anyone.
In Russian, goodbye goes with **ты** and **вы**: **Пока́** only to people
you call ты. To Vera Ivanovna, a doctor or a cashier, **До свида́ния**. **До
встре́чи** works for everyone.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, with the soft ending wherever there's a ь.*

> брат — брать · у́гол — у́голь · сел — съел
> день — фильм — подъе́зд — О́льга — И́горь

### Б. Пока́ и́ли До свида́ния?

*How do you say goodbye?*

1. To a friend.
2. To Vera Ivanovna, the elderly neighbour.
3. To the doctor.
4. To your brother, at night, before going to bed.
5. To a colleague you'll see tomorrow.

### В. Что отве́тить?

*What do you answer?*

1. — Как дела́? — …
2. — Спаси́бо! — …
3. — Пока́! — …
4. — Ко́фе и́ли чай? — …

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**The whole alphabet, from memory.** Write all 33 letters by hand, in order,
capital and small, without looking. Then check them against the table on
day 7 and mark the ones you forgot: those are the ones to go over tomorrow.

And say goodbye aloud to five imaginary people, choosing each time between
**Пока́** and **До свида́ния**.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> Пока́! · До встре́чи! · До свида́ния! · Споко́йной но́чи! · Норма́льно.
>
> **I know:** all 33 letters. I can read any Russian word.

Tomorrow: the unit 1 test. And the day after, the first episode of the
story.

## Answers

**А.** **О́льга** and **И́горь** are Russian names; in both, the consonant
before the ь is soft.

**Б.** 1 Пока́! · 2 До свида́ния! · 3 До свида́ния! · 4 Споко́йной но́чи! · 5
Пока́! or До встре́чи!

**В.** 1 Хорошо́. / Норма́льно. / Пло́хо. · 2 Пожа́луйста. · 3 Пока́! · 4
Чай, пожа́луйста. (or Ко́фе, пожа́луйста.)
