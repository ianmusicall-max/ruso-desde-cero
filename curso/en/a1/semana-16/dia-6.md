## Answers

**1. Vocabulary:** 1 а (week 15, day 1) · 2 в (week 15, day 5) · 3 б

**2. Grammar:** 6 б (week 15, day 2) · 7 в (week 15, day 3) · 8 а (week

**3. Dialogues:** 11 б (week 16, day 1) · 12 б (week 16, day 3) · 13 а

**The traps.** In 6 and 7, the OBJECT's gender decides the ending:
му́зыка is feminine and changes; журна́л is masculine and doesn't. In
10, **смотре́ть** follows neither the **чита́ть** nor the **слу́шать**
pattern: compare **чита́ешь** with **смо́тришь**. In 12, **ненави́жу**
is much stronger than **не люблю́**: they aren't the same sentence
with different words. In 13, the **д** in **по́здно** drops out of the
**-здн-** cluster, just like in **пра́здник**.
