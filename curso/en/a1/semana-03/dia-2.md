> **Today** you find out why **метро́** sounds [mi-TRO]: unstressed е and я weaken. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read unstressed е and я correctly;
> - name seven things you need in class: **тетра́дь**, **каранда́ш**, **слова́рь**…;
> - tell a **вопро́с** from an **отве́т**.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Correct each one aloud, with **не…, а…**:

> Э́то кни́га? (журна́л) · Э́то зонт? (ла́мпа) · Э́то ключ? (ру́чка)

(Нет, э́то не кни́га, а журна́л. · Нет, э́то не зонт, а ла́мпа. · Нет, э́то не
ключ, а ру́чка.)

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

You already know that unstressed о sounds [a]. Today, its cousins:
**unstressed е and я sound like a short [i]**.

| Stressed: full sound | Unstressed: short [i] |
|---|---|
| **нет** [nyet] | **метро́** [mi-TRO] |
| **день** [dyen'] | **телефо́н** [ti-li-FON] |
| **я́хта** [YAKH-ta] | **язы́к** [i-ZYK] |
| **меня́** [mi-NYA] | **рестора́н** [ri-sta-RAN] |

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat. Find the stressed syllable first: the other е's and я's fade.*

> метро́ — телефо́н — рестора́н — тетра́дь — язы́к
> нет — день — я́хта — отве́т

You already knew many of these words. Now you know how they really sound.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

Things for class:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| тетра́дь | ti-**TRAT'** | exercise book, notebook |
| каранда́ш | ka-ran-**DASH** | pencil |
| рюкза́к | ryoog-**ZAK** | backpack, rucksack |
| слова́рь | sla-**VAR'** | dictionary |
| уро́к | oo-**ROK** | lesson, class |
| вопро́с | va-**PROS** | question |
| отве́т | at-**VYET** | answer |

Look at **тетра́дь**: the first е is unstressed and sounds [i]; and the final
д sounds almost like a t. That's the rule from the day after tomorrow:
you can see it coming.

> — Что э́то?
> *What's this?*
> — Э́то тетра́дь.
> *An exercise book.*
> — А э́то?
> *And this?*
> — Э́то не тетра́дь, а слова́рь.
> *It's not an exercise book, it's a dictionary.*

**Вопро́с** is the question and **отве́т** the answer: two words you'll see in
every exercise of the course.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**Same idea as English, different sound.** English weakens unstressed vowels
too, but to a neutral *uh* (*telephone* is TEL-*uh*-phone). Russian weakens
е and я to a short **[i]**, not to *uh*:

| Word | An English mouth says | It's said |
|---|---|---|
| телефо́н | ~~[TEL-uh-fon]~~ | [ti-li-FON] |
| метро́ | ~~[MET-roh]~~ | [mi-TRO] |
| тетра́дь | ~~[TET-rad]~~ | [ti-TRAT'] |

And notice the stress again: *TELephone* leans on the first syllable;
**телефо́н** on the last.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Как говори́м?

*How does the bold vowel sound: full, or like [и]?*

1. т**е**лефо́н
2. д**е**нь
3. м**е**тро́
4. **я**зы́к
5. отв**е́**т
6. м**е**ня́

### Б. Соедини́те.

*Match each word with its translation.*

1. тетра́дь
2. каранда́ш
3. рюкза́к
4. слова́рь
5. вопро́с

а) backpack · б) question · в) exercise book · г) dictionary · д) pencil

### В. Вопро́с и́ли отве́т?

*Is it a question or an answer?*

1. Что э́то?
2. Э́то слова́рь.
3. Э́то тетра́дь?
4. Нет, э́то не тетра́дь.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your desk.** Point at what's in front of you and name it aloud, with a
mistake or two on purpose that you then correct:

> Э́то тетра́дь. Э́то не ру́чка, а каранда́ш. А э́то? Э́то слова́рь.

Record yourself and listen to the е's: do the unstressed ones fade? Then
write the seven words by hand and underline the stressed syllable.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> тетра́дь, каранда́ш, рюкза́к, слова́рь · Э́то не тетра́дь, а слова́рь.
>
> **I know:** unstressed е and я sound [и]: метро́ [mi-TRO].
