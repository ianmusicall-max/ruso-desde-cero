> **Today** you learn to tell apart two sounds that sound a lot alike. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - pronounce **ч** and **щ** without mixing them up;
> - recognize three new words with those sounds;
> - use the length of **щ** as a clue.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

A word with **ч** you already know, out loud:

> ключ

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Ч** sounds like English "ch." **Щ** doesn't exist in English: it's
close, but longer, as if two consonants were squeezed into one.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat, first the ones with ч and then the ones with щ:*

> час — чай — ключ
> щи — вещь — ещё

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**You already know чай from the unit-2 café, and ещё from unit 3**
(**уже́** and **ещё**, already and still); today three more words join
them, split between the two sounds.

| Word | It sounds like | It means |
|---|---|---|
| час | chas | hour |
| чай | chay | tea |
| щи | **shchi** | cabbage soup |
| вещь | **véshch'** | thing, object |
| ещё | yi-**SHCHO** | still, more |

**Щи** is the name of a Russian soup, and here it only serves to
practice the initial sound. **Length helps**: **щ** can be held for a
moment, like a long hiss; **ч** starts sharply and doesn't stretch.

> — У тебя́ есть час?
> *Do you have an hour (free)?*
> — Да, оди́н час. Мне чай, пожа́луйста.
> *Yes, one hour. Tea for me, please.*
> — Вот, пожа́луйста. А э́то чья вещь?
> *Here you go. And whose thing is this?*
> — Моя́. Ещё чай, пожа́луйста.
> *Mine. More tea, please.*
> — Да, ещё.
> *Yes, more.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English has no sound like щ, so the temptation is to swap it for ч.**
They're two separate sounds to a Russian ear, even if at first they
sound the same to you:

| Word | Don't confuse it with |
|---|---|
| щи (soup) | чи (that word doesn't exist) |
| вещь (thing) | *say "вечь" and it won't be understood* |

The way to keep them apart when speaking is to remember the length:
**щ** can be "stretched" a little; **ч** can't.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, slowly the first time.*

> час · чай · ключ · щи · вещь · ещё

### Б. Ч и́ли щ?

*Which sound does this word start with, or contain?*

1. чай → а) ч · б) щ
2. щи → а) ч · б) щ
3. вещь → а) ч · б) щ
4. ключ → а) ч · б) щ

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Мне чай, пожа́луйста.
2. Ещё, пожа́луйста.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Record yourself.** Read the six words from "Pronunciation" and
"Warm-up" aloud, then listen back: can you hear the difference between
ч and щ?

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Мне чай, пожа́луйста. · Э́то чья вещь? · Ещё, пожа́луйста.
>
> **Now I know:** how to tell **ч** and **щ** apart by length, and three new words with those sounds.
