What someone used to be

Он был студе́нтом

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

1Warm-up0–3 min

Recall two sentences from yesterday. Find a form you already recognise in today’s heading.

Он был студе́нтом

2Pronunciation3–8 min

Read the heading slowly and mark the stressed syllables. Say it as one phrase without giving every word the same weight. Record the first line and repeat just the part you found hard.

Он был студе́нтом.

3New8–18 min

Read the examples with был or была́ for past occupations. Learn the job form as part of the sentence rather than a rule for other adjectives.

Он был студе́нтом.
Она́ была́ врачо́м.
Он был ги́дом.

⚠️ Contrast with English

English says ‘work as a doctor’. Russian uses врачо́м after рабо́таю; return to врач in a direct introduction with я.

4Practice18–25 min

А. Скажи́те по-ру́сски.

Cover the Russian and reconstruct the three sentences. Then check the key.

  1. He was a guide.
  2. He was a student.
  3. She was a doctor.

Б. Измени́те фра́зу.

Read the three models. Change one detail in each and check whether the word order or an ending must change too.

5Your turn25–29 min

Describe two characters then and now. Keep the forms for the present and the past distinct.

6Wrap-up29–30 min

Recall one full sentence without looking. Explain what it means and identify a word that can change when adapting it to a different person or situation.
