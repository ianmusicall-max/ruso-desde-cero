> **Today** you learn two short verbs with two different kinds of irregularity. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **пе́ть** (пою́), which changes completely;
> - use **спа́ть** (сплю́), from the гото́вить family;
> - tell the two irregularities apart.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's verb, from memory:

> гото́влю · гото́вишь · гото́вит

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Пою́** and **поёшь** carry a hidden **й** between two vowels, which
isn't written but sounds like a quick "y."

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> пою́ — поёшь — поёт

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Пе́ть changes its whole root: there's no rule that predicts it, you
just have to learn it.** It's different from писа́ть's с → ш change
and from the disappearing `-ова-`: here the whole word transforms.

| Infinitive | я | ты | он, она́ |
|---|---|---|---|
| пе́ть | по**ю́** | по**ёшь** | по**ёт** |

**Спа́ть, on the other hand, is from the гото́вить family**: it adds
an л in "я" and doesn't shift the stress in the other forms.

| Infinitive | я | ты | он, она́ |
|---|---|---|---|
| спа́ть | с**пл**ю́ | спишь | спит |

> — Ты поёшь пе́сню?
> *Are you singing a song?*
> — Да, пою́. А пото́м я сплю́.
> *Yes, I am. And then I sleep.*
> — Он спит сейча́с?
> *Is he sleeping now?*
> — Нет, он не спит, он поёт!
> *No, he's not sleeping, he's singing!*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also has fully irregular verbs ("am," not "be-s"); Russian
has them too, and пе́ть is one of them.** The important thing is not
to look for a rule that doesn't exist:

| A rule that DOES exist (спа́ть) | No rule, has to be memorized (пе́ть) |
|---|---|
| с**пл**ю́, like гото́влю | по**ю́**, a different root |

When a verb doesn't follow any pattern you know, the only strategy
is to repeat it out loud until it sounds natural.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я пою́. Ты спишь? Он поёт пе́сню.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. I sing.
2. Are you sleeping?
3. He's singing a song.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ты поёшь пе́сню?
2. Она́ не спит, она́ поёт.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your night routine.** Say out loud whether you поёшь before you
спа́ть, and which пе́сню you'd sing.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Я пою́. · Ты спишь? · Он поёт пе́сню.
>
> **Now I know:** пе́ть is root-irregular, with no rule; спа́ть follows гото́вить's pattern.
