> **Today** you learn the location ending for countries in `-ия`. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **в Росси́и**, **в Ита́лии**, and **в Испа́нии**;
> - use **во Фра́нции**, with the **во** that changes;
> - name **Ита́лия** and **Фра́нция**, the two new ones.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The four posture verbs, from memory:

> сиди́т · стои́т · лежи́т · виси́т

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Росси́и**, **Ита́лии**, and **Испа́нии** end in two **и**'s in a
row, pronounced as one long vowel.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> в Росси́и — в Ита́лии — в Испа́нии — во Фра́нции

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Countries that end in `-ия` change that ending to `-ии` when saying WHERE**, with a new group of proper nouns:

| Country (dictionary) | In the country (place) |
|---|---|
| Росси́я | в Росси́**и** |
| Ита́лия | в Ита́л**ии** |
| Испа́ния | в Испа́н**ии** |

> Том в Ита́лии.
> *Tom is in Italy.*
> Ками́ла в Испа́нии.
> *Camila is in Spain.*
> Ки́ра в Росси́и.
> *Kira is in Russia.*

**Фра́нция doesn't end in `-ия`, but in `-ция`, and it also changes
the preposition: it's not «в», it's «во».**

> Ми́ша во Фра́нции.
> *Misha is in France.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English uses "in" for any country, without changing its name;
Russian changes the country's ending AND, sometimes, even the
preposition itself.**

| English (fixed name) | Russian (name and sometimes preposition change) |
|---|---|
| IN France | во Фра́нции |
| IN Italy | в Ита́лии |

**Во** instead of **в** exists only so the phrase sounds better:
"в Фра́нции" piles up too many consonants in a row. It's not a rule
you can always predict; learn it with this specific word.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Том в Ита́лии. Ками́ла в Испа́нии. Ми́ша во Фра́нции.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. Tom is in Italy.
2. Kira is in Russia.
3. Misha is in France.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ками́ла в Испа́нии.
2. Ты в Росси́и?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Four countries.** Say out loud where each character is
(imaginarily): Том, Ками́ла, Ки́ра, and Ми́ша, using one of today's
four countries.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Том в Ита́лии. · Ками́ла в Испа́нии. · Ми́ша во Фра́нции.
>
> **Now I know:** countries ending in `-ия` change to `-ии`, and Фра́нция also changes the preposition to **во**.
