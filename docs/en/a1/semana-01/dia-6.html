A1 · Unit 1 · Week 1 · Day 6 · Review

Review: read everything from this week

Повторе́ние: чита́ем всё

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today there's nothing new: you put together the week's 15 letters, words and phrases, and test yourself. By the end you'll know:

  • which letters you read without thinking and which still fool you;
  • whether your question sounds like a question;
  • how much of the mini-test you get right without looking anything up.

1Warm-up0–3 min

The week's 15 letters, in the order they arrived. Say the sound of each one aloud:

А О К М Т · С Р У Х · В Е Н · И · Э Д

Now just the tricky ones. Which one still fools you? That's the one you look at twice today:

С Р У Х В Е Н И

2Pronunciation3–8 min

The week's three rules, out loud.

An unstressed о sounds [a]. Find the stressed о first:

вода́ — окно́ — Москва́ — ко́смос — до́ктор — ко́мната

Е and и soften the consonant in front of them:

нет — не — ни — ди — те — ти

A statement falls; a question jumps up on the stressed syllable and drops right after:

Э́то вода́. — Э́то вода́?
Там метро́. — Там метро́?
Э́то ко́мната. — Э́то ко́мната?

3Mini-test8–23 min

Don't look back at the earlier days. Choose one answer for each question: on the website, tap it; on paper, note the letter. The key is at the end.

1. Reading

How is it said?

  1. сок → а) [kok] · б) [sok] · в) [sak]
  2. рука́ → а) [poo-KA] · б) [roo-KA] · в) [ROO-ka]
  3. нет → а) [het] · б) [net] · в) [nyet]
  4. вот → а) [bot] · б) [vot] · в) [vat]
  5. Москва́ → а) [mos-KVA] · б) [MAS-kva] · в) [mas-KVA]

2. Words

What does it mean?

  1. у́тро → а) morning · б) ear · в) hand
  2. окно́ → а) window · б) room · в) cinema
  3. дива́н → а) house · б) sofa · в) water
  4. ка́рта → а) cake · б) map · в) mask
  5. вода́ → а) juice · б) sofa · в) water

3. Dialogues and intonation

Choose the reply that fits.

  1. — Как тебя́ зову́т? — … → а) О́чень прия́тно. · б) Да, э́то Ни́на. · в) Меня́ зову́т Ни́на.
  2. — Меня́ зову́т Анто́н. А тебя́? — … → а) Мари́на. · б) Нет, э́то кот. · в) Как тебя́ зову́т?
  3. — Кто э́то? — … → а) Э́то вода́. · б) Э́то дом. · в) Э́то А́нна.
  4. — Что э́то? — … → а) Э́то дива́н. · б) Э́то Анто́н. · в) Да, э́то Анто́н.
  5. You want to ask "is it a room?". Where does your voice jump in Э́то ко́мната? → а) on "ко́", dropping on the rest of the word · б) on "та", at the end · в) on "Э́"

Count your right answers. With 13 or more, the week is done. With 10 to 12, go back to the days the key points to for each mistake. With fewer than 10, reread days 2 and 3 before moving on: everything else is built on them.

4Your turn23–29 min

Your first card. Write by hand, in Cyrillic, a four-line dialogue: someone says hi and asks your name, you answer and ask theirs, and you both finish with О́чень прия́тно. Use names you can write with the 15 letters: А́нна, Анто́н, Макси́м, Мари́на, Ни́на, Ве́ра.

Then read it aloud and record it. Listen for two things: does зову́т sound [za-VOOT]? Does the question jump where it should?

To finish, write the 15 letters from memory, capital and small.

5Wrap-up29–30 min

Mini-test: 1 б (day 2) · 2 б (day 2) · 3 в (day 3) · 4 б (day 3) · 5 в (day 3) · 6 а (day 2) · 7 а (day 3) · 8 б (day 5) · 9 б (day 2) · 10 в (day 5) · 11 в (day 4) · 12 а (day 4) · 13 в (day 5) · 14 а (day 5) · 15 а (day 5)

The traps. In 3, Н is an n, not an h, and е softens it: [nyet]. In 4, вот has one syllable, so its о is stressed and sounds [o]. In 5, the unstressed о sounds [a] and the stress is at the end. In 15, if your voice jumps on "Э́" you're asking something else: "is this the room?".
