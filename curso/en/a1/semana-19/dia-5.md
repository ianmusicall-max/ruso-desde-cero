> **Today** you learn the second prepositional ending, only for a group of masculine nouns. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **в шкафу́**, **в углу́**, **на полу́**, and **в саду́**;
> - name **пол**, the only new word;
> - tell this ending apart from the **-е** you already know.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's chunk, from memory:

> на ку́хне · на балко́не · в коридо́ре

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Шкафу́**, **углу́**, **полу́**, and **саду́** are always stressed on
the last syllable, on **-у́**.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> в шкафу́ — в углу́ — на полу́ — в саду́

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Some masculine nouns don't use -е in the prepositional: they use
-у́, always stressed.** There's no rule that predicts it from the
word itself; they're learned as a closed group, one by one.

| Dictionary form | Where (with stressed -у́) |
|---|---|
| шкаф | в шкаф**у́** |
| у́гол | в угл**у́** |
| пол | на пол**у́** |
| сад | в сад**у́** |

> — Где су́мка?
> *Where's the bag?*
> — Су́мка в шкафу́. А стул в углу́.
> *The bag is in the cupboard. And the chair is in the corner.*
> — А ключи́?
> *And the keys?*
> — Ключи́ на полу́! А Том в саду́.
> *The keys are on the floor! And Tom is in the garden.*

**You already know сад**: remember that its plural is **сады́**,
with the same kind of stress on the end. That's not a coincidence:
this noun shifts the stress in several of its forms.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English has nothing like this closed list of words with a
different ending; each one is learned on its own, not by a rule.**

| With **-е** (most nouns) | With **-у́** (closed group) |
|---|---|
| в до́ме, на столе́ | в шкафу́, на полу́ |

If you make a mistake and say `в шкафе`, you'll be understood, but
this list —шкаф, у́гол, пол, сад, and a few more you'll meet later—
always takes **-у́**.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Су́мка в шкафу́. Стул в углу́. Ключи́ на полу́.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. The bag is in the cupboard.
2. The keys are on the floor.
3. Tom is in the garden.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Где стул? Стул в углу́.
2. Телефо́н на полу́, а не на столе́.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Four places with -у́.** Say out loud what's there (or who is) в
шкафу́, в углу́, на полу́, and в саду́.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Су́мка в шкафу́. · Стул в углу́. · Ключи́ на полу́.
>
> **Now I know:** a closed group of masculine nouns that use stressed **-у́** instead of **-е**.

## Answers

**Б.** 1 Су́мка в шкафу́. · 2 Ключи́ на полу́. · 3 Том в саду́.

**В.** 1 Where's the chair? The chair is in the corner. · 2 The
phone is on the floor, and not on the table.
