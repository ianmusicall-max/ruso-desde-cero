A1 · Unit 1 · Week 2 · Day 1 · Lesson

Б, П, Г, Л. How are you?

Б, П, Г, Л. Как дела́?

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you learn four letters with no tricks and how to ask how someone is. By the end you'll be able to:

  • read Б, П, Г, Л;
  • ask Как дела́? and answer Хорошо́, Пло́хо or То́же хорошо́;
  • say thank you: Спаси́бо.

1Warm-up0–3 min

Read aloud and decide whether each line is a question or a statement, raising or lowering your voice:

Э́то кот? — Да, э́то кот. · Кто э́то? — Э́то Анто́н. · Что э́то? — Э́то дива́н.

2Pronunciation3–8 min

Four new letters. This time they don't lie: they sound pretty much as you'd expect.

Letter Sounds As in
Б б [b] big
П п [p] spin (no puff of air)
Г г [g] go, always
Л л [l] the "dark" l of full

Слу́шайте и повторя́йте. Listen and repeat:

ба — бо — бу · па — по — пу
га — го — гу — ги · ла — ло — лу

Г is always the g of go, even before е and и, where English sometimes turns it into a j sound (gin, gem): гита́ра is [gi-TA-ra], never "jee-tara".

Your l is already Russian. The l at the end of full or milk, with the back of the tongue raised, is exactly the hard л. Use that one everywhere, at the start of words too: ла, ло, лу.

And п has no puff, like к and т on day 1: hold the paper in front of your mouth and say па́па. It should barely move.

3New8–18 min

With 19 letters, more words:

Word Say it Means
па́па PA-pa dad
бана́н ba-NAN banana
парк park park
гита́ра gi-TA-ra guitar
пло́хо PLO-kha badly, bad
спаси́бо spa-SEE-ba thank you

And today's dialogue. Two friends bump into each other in the street:

— Приве́т, Анто́н! Как дела́?
Hi, Anton! How are things?
— Хорошо́. А у тебя́?
Fine. And you?
— То́же хорошо́, спаси́бо.
Fine too, thanks.

You can read all of Как дела́? already: [kak di-LA]. Хорошо́ and то́же have letters from the day after tomorrow; learn them by ear:

Phrase Say it Means
Как дела́? kak di-LA How are things?
Хорошо́. kha-ra-SHO Fine. Good.
А у тебя́? a oo ti-BYA And you?
То́же TO-zhe too, also

Look at хорошо́: three o's, and only the last one is stressed. The other two sound [a]. Say [kho-ro-SHO] and you'll be understood, but it gives you away.

⚠️ Contrast with English

Как дела́? isn't "How are you?". In English how are you? is a greeting: you say it to the cashier, and the answer is always fine. In Russian Как дела́? is for people you know — friends, colleagues, neighbours — and it's a real question: the answer doesn't have to be "fine". You don't ask a stranger.

Who Как дела́?
the cashier, someone you don't know no: just greet them (the polite greeting, Здра́вствуйте, comes in unit 3)
a friend, a colleague, a neighbour yes, and wait for the answer

And г is never a j: гита́ра, not "jee-tara".

4Practice18–25 min

А. Чита́йте.

Read aloud. No traps today: just new letters.

бана́н — парк — суп — спорт — лимо́н — гита́ра — бале́т — па́па — план — Ли́ма

Б. Допиши́те диало́г.

Complete the dialogue. You can copy the words from the lesson.

— Приве́т! Как …?
— Хорошо́. А у …?
— … хорошо́, спаси́бо.

В. Соедини́те.

Match each word with its translation.

  1. па́па
  2. бана́н
  3. парк
  4. гита́ра
  5. пло́хо

а) park · б) guitar · в) dad · г) badly · д) banana

5Your turn25–29 min

Three meetings, three answers. Imagine you bump into three people you know. Ask Как дела́? and answer yourself differently each time:

— Как дела́? — Хорошо́, спаси́бо. А у тебя́? — То́же хорошо́.
— Как дела́? — Пло́хо… — Пло́хо?
— Как дела́? — Хорошо́! А у тебя́? — Пло́хо.

Record yourself and listen: does хорошо́ sound [kha-ra-SHO]? Then write Б, П, Г, Л by hand, capital and small.

6Wrap-up29–30 min

I can now say:
Как дела́? · Хорошо́. · Пло́хо. · А у тебя́? · То́же хорошо́, спаси́бо.

I know: 19 of 33 letters. 14 to go.
