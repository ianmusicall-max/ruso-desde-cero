> **Today** you discover that not every place follows yesterday's logic. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **на ку́хне** and **на балко́не**, two exceptions you learn with their preposition;
> - use **коридо́р**, the new word that does follow the rule;
> - know when to distrust the inside/surface logic.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's chunk, from memory:

> на столе́ · на по́лке · на дива́не

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Балко́не** is stressed on the same syllable as **балко́н**;
**коридо́ре** is too.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> на ку́хне — на балко́не — в коридо́ре

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Ку́хня is a room, INSIDE the house, but Russian uses на, not в.**
There's no logical reason: it's an exception you learn by heart,
as a fixed chunk.

| Place | Preposition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ку́хня | **на** ку́хне | exception, learn it as is |
| балко́н | **на** балко́не | exception, learn it as is |
| коридо́р | **в** коридо́ре | follows yesterday's rule |
| спа́льня | **в** спа́льне | follows yesterday's rule |

> — Где ты?
> *Where are you?*
> — Я на ку́хне. А Ки́ра на балко́не.
> *I'm in the kitchen. And Kira is on the balcony.*
> — А Том?
> *And Tom?*
> — Том в коридо́ре. Ми́ша в спа́льне.
> *Tom is in the hallway. Misha is in the bedroom.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also has prepositions that can't be predicted by logic
—"at the corner" but "on the table," with nothing to explain it—
so this irregularity isn't foreign to you.** The only difference is
which words fall into the irregular group: in Russian, **ку́хня**
and **балко́н** are in it; in English, they aren't.

| What DOES follow the logic | What does NOT |
|---|---|
| **в** коридо́ре (inside) | **на** ку́хне (inside, but with "на") |

When a place word doesn't behave as expected, just learn it with
its preposition, the way you already did with на гита́ре in unit 9.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я на ку́хне. Ки́ра на балко́не. Том в коридо́ре.

### Б. В и́ли на?

*Choose the correct preposition.*

1. Ми́ша ______ ку́хне.
2. Том ______ коридо́ре.
3. Ками́ла ______ балко́не.
4. Я ______ спа́льне.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ками́ла то́же на ку́хне.
2. Том в коридо́ре, а Ми́ша в спа́льне.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Four places.** Say out loud which place in the house you're in
right now, choosing from today's four.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Я на ку́хне. · Ки́ра на балко́не. · Том в коридо́ре.
>
> **Now I know:** **ку́хня** and **балко́н** are exceptions with **на**, and **коридо́р** follows the rule with **в**.
