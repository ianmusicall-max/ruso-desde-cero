> **Today** you find out why **хлеб** sounds [khlyep]: at the end of a word, voiced consonants lose their voice. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read word endings correctly;
> - know why **код** and **кот** sound the same;
> - name eight new words: **хлеб**, **сад**, **снег**, **моро́з**…

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Read each pair, first hard, then soft:

> лук — люк · рад — ряд · брат — брать · у́гол — у́голь

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Six Russian consonants come in pairs: the same mouth position, with voice
and without. Put your hand on your throat to check: with voice, it buzzes.

| Voiced | б | в | г | д | ж | з |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Voiceless** | п | ф | к | т | ш | с |

**The rule: at the end of a word, the top one sounds like the bottom one.**
You write **хлеб** and say [khlyep]; you write **сад** and say [sat].

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat. At the end, no voice.*

> хлеб [khlyep] · сад [sat] · нож [nosh] · глаз [glas] · снег [snyek] · зуб [zoop]

That's why **код** and **кот** sound exactly the same: [kot]. And that's why
surnames in **-ов** sound [-of]: **Ивано́в** is [i-va-NOF].

## 3. New (8–18 min)

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| хлеб | khlyep | bread |
| сад | sat | garden |
| нож | nosh | knife |
| глаз | glas | eye |
| снег | snyek | snow |
| зуб | zoop | tooth |
| гриб | greep | mushroom |
| моро́з | ma-**ROS** | frost, hard cold |

> — Что э́то?
> *What's this?*
> — Э́то хлеб.
> *Bread.*
> — А э́то? Сыр?
> *And this? Cheese?*
> — Нет, э́то не сыр, а гриб!
> *No, it's not cheese, it's a mushroom!*

The rule works **only at the end**. If a vowel follows, the consonant gets its
voice back: **сад** [sat], but **сады́** (gardens) [sa-DY]. That's why Russian
writes the real letter even when you can't hear it: so you recognise the
word in all its forms.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English keeps its final voice; Russian drops it.** In English *bad* and
*bat*, *dog* and *dock* are different words, and you've been voicing final
*b*, *d*, *g* all your life. In Russian you mustn't:

| Word | ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|---|
| сад | ~~[sad]~~ (like *sad*) | [sat] (like *sat*) |
| хлеб | ~~[khlyeb]~~ | [khlyep] |
| друг (friend) | ~~[droog]~~ | [drook] |

And don't stretch the vowel either: English makes the vowel of *bad* longer
than the vowel of *bat*. In **сад** the а is short, as in *sat*.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud. No voice at the end of each word.*

> хлеб — сад — нож — глаз — снег — зуб — гриб — моро́з — Ивано́в

### Б. Как говори́м?

*What sound do you hear at the end?*

1. хлеб
2. сад
3. нож
4. глаз
5. снег
6. Ивано́в

### В. Одина́ково и́ли нет?

*Do they sound the same or not?*

1. код — кот
2. луг (meadow) — лук
3. дом — том (volume)
4. гриб — грипп (flu)

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**The shopping list.** Read it aloud, slowly, and record yourself:

> Хлеб, сыр, мя́со, лук, соль… и гриб!

Listen to the endings: do **хлеб** [khlyep] and **гриб** [greep] come out with
no voice? Read the list again, this time as questions, item by item: **Хлеб?
Сыр? Мя́со?**

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> хлеб, сад, нож, глаз, снег, зуб, гриб, моро́з · Э́то не сыр, а гриб!
>
> **I know:** at the end of a word, б, в, г, д, ж, з sound п, ф, к, т, ш, с.
