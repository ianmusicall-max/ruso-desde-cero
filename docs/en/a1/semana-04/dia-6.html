Unit 2 test: reading Russian

Тест: чита́ем по-ру́сски

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you check the whole of unit 2: reading by the rules, understanding the words and answering with the right intonation. By the end you'll know what's solid and which days to go back to before unit 3.

1Instructions0–2 min

Fifteen questions in three blocks, each with four answers and only one right one. Don't look at the lessons and don't use a dictionary: if you don't know something, skip it and move on. On the website, tap the answer; on paper, note the letter. You have 23 minutes, more than enough; if you finish early, read block 1 aloud.

2Test2–25 min

1. Чте́ние

How is it said?

  1. молоко́ → а) [mo-lo-KO] · б) [ma-LO-ka] · в) [ma-la-KO] · г) [MO-la-ka]
  2. тетра́дь → а) [te-TRAD] · б) [ti-TRAD'] · в) [ti-TRAT] · г) [ti-TRAT']
  3. сего́дня → а) [si-VOD-nya] · б) [se-GOD-nya] · в) [si-GOD-nya] · г) [syi-vad-NYA]
  4. дверь → а) [dvyer'] · б) [dvyer] · в) [dver] · г) [dve-REE]
  5. авто́бус → а) [af-ta-BOOS] · б) [af-TO-boos] · в) [av-TO-boos] · г) [AF-ta-boos]

2. Ле́ксика

What does it mean?

  1. ключ → а) pen · б) key · в) umbrella · г) lamp
  2. сосе́дка → а) neighbour (a woman) · б) friend (a woman) · в) girl · г) mother
  3. так себе́ → а) very well · б) bad · в) so-so · г) great
  4. фами́лия → а) family · б) first name · в) shop · г) surname
  5. наве́рное → а) of course · б) I don't know · в) OK · г) probably

3. Диало́ги и интона́ция

Choose the reply that fits.

  1. — Что вам? — … → а) Мне чай, пожа́луйста. · б) Я чай. · в) Вот, пожа́луйста. · г) Спаси́бо.
  2. — Как дела́? — Пло́хо. — … → а) Здо́рово! · б) Отли́чно! · в) Что случи́лось? · г) Вот, пожа́луйста.
  3. — Э́то кни́га? — … → а) Нет, э́то нет кни́га. · б) Не, э́то тетра́дь. · в) Да, э́то не кни́га. · г) Нет, э́то не кни́га, а тетра́дь.
  4. — Здра́вствуйте! Как дела́? — Хорошо́, спаси́бо. … → а) А у тебя́? · б) А у вас? · в) Приве́т! · г) Не зна́ю.
  5. Which one is said with ИК-4, the voice rising and staying up? → а) Кто э́то? · б) А вы? · в) Э́то вы. · г) Что э́то?

4What to review29–30 min

Note the days of your mistakes. If you got two or more wrong in block 1, review the whole of week 3 first, in the order the rules came: unstressed е and я (day 2), hard and soft (day 3) and the final consonant losing its voice (day 4).

Show the answers

1. Чте́ние: 1 в (wk 4, day 2) · 2 г (wk 3, day 2) · 3 а (wk 4, day 5) · 4 а (wk 3, day 3) · 5 б (wk 4, day 3)

2. Ле́ксика: 6 б (wk 3, day 1) · 7 а (wk 3, day 5) · 8 в (wk 4, day 1) · 9 г (wk 3, day 7) · 10 г (wk 4, day 4)

3. Диало́ги и интона́ция: 11 а (wk 4, day 2) · 12 в (wk 4, day 1) · 13 г (wk 3, day 1) · 14 б (wk 4, day 1) · 15 б (wk 3, day 5)

The traps. In 1, of the three о's in молоко́ only the last is stressed: the other two sound [a]. In 2 there are three rules at once: the unstressed е sounds [i], the final д loses its voice and the ь keeps it soft. In 3, the г of сего́дня sounds [v]. In 4, you can hear the ь: the final р is soft. In 5, the stress isn't at the front as in English, and the в sounds [f]. In 9, фами́лия is the surname; family is семья́. In 10, наве́рное doesn't promise: it's "probably", not "of course". In 11, you order with no verb and no "I": Мне чай. In 13, нет answers and не negates the word after it. In 14, once you start on вы, you stay on вы.
