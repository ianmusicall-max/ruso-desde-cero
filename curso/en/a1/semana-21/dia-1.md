> **Today** you start the unit that describes HOW something is in its place, not just WHERE. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **сиди́т** for a person sitting;
> - use **стои́т** for a person standing;
> - combine both with the places you already know.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Last unit's chunk, from memory:

> в гости́ной · на ку́хне · в коридо́ре

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Сиди́т** and **стои́т** are stressed on the last syllable, and the
final **и** sounds clear in both.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> сиди́т — стои́т

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Until now you said WHERE someone is; today you add HOW they are:
sitting or standing.** The posture verb comes first, then the
place, with the ending you already know:

| Posture | He/she |
|---|---|
| sitting | **сиди́т** |
| standing | **стои́т** |

> Он сиди́т на сту́ле.
> *He is sitting on the chair.*
> Она́ стои́т на ку́хне.
> *She is standing in the kitchen.*
> Ми́ша сиди́т на дива́не.
> *Misha is sitting on the sofa.*
> Том стои́т в коридо́ре.
> *Tom is standing in the hallway.*

> — Где сиди́т Ми́ша?
> *Where is Misha sitting?*
> — Ки́ра сиди́т в гости́ной.
> *Kira is sitting in the living room.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English uses "to be" + a participle ("is sitting," "is standing")
to describe posture; Russian uses its OWN VERB for each posture,
with no "to be" at all.**

| English ("to be" + participle) | Russian (its own verb) |
|---|---|
| IS sitting | **сиди́т** |
| IS standing | **стои́т** |

Don't look for a "to be" in the Russian sentence: сиди́т and стои́т
already carry everything you need to say.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Он сиди́т на сту́ле. Она́ стои́т на ку́хне. Ми́ша сиди́т на дива́не.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. He is sitting on the chair.
2. She is standing in the hallway.
3. Where is Misha sitting?

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Том стои́т в коридо́ре.
2. Ки́ра сиди́т в гости́ной.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your posture now.** Say out loud whether you're **сиди́шь** or
**стои́шь** right now, and in which room.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Он сиди́т на сту́ле. · Она́ стои́т на ку́хне. · Где сиди́т Ми́ша?
>
> **Now I know:** **сиди́т** and **стои́т** describe posture, with no "to be," and combine with the place you already know.
