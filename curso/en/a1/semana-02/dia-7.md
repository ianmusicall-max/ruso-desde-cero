> **Today** the story that runs through the whole course begins. You read all of it in Russian, using only what you've learnt in these two weeks. By the end you'll have met Camila, Tom and Kira, and found two mistakes: one of them is yours.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

Three characters, and a cat:

- **Ками́ла** (Camila), 26, from Lima. She's a cook, and she arrives in Moscow
  today on a scholarship, to study Russian for a year and then do a master's.
- **Том** (Tom), 31, from Toronto. He teaches English and has lived in the
  building for a month.
- **Ки́ра** (Kira), a doctor, a neighbour in the same building. She lives with
  her son, Misha, and with **Ба́рсик** (Barsik), a cat who turns up where he
  shouldn't.

Camila makes the mistakes of a Spanish speaker; Tom makes those of an English
speaker. Today there's one of each. Before you read, jot down three words
from the unit you think they'll say.

**The entrance.** In Russian cities most people live in blocks of flats, and
the **подъе́зд**, the entrance and stairwell, is where neighbours bump into
each other every day. That's where it all starts.

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Эпизо́д 1. Знако́мство

*A September afternoon. The entrance of a block of flats in a Moscow neighbourhood. Camila arrives with an enormous suitcase. A cat dashes out of the stairwell.*

> **Ками́ла:** Ой! Кот! Кто э́то?
> **Ки́ра:** Ба́рсик! Ба́рсик, нет! Извини́те!
> **Ками́ла:** Ничего́!
> **Ки́ра:** Я Ки́ра. А ты?
> **Ками́ла:** Я есть Ками́ла.
> **Ки́ра:** Ками́ла? О́чень прия́тно!

*Tom comes down the stairs, a guitar on his back.*

> **Ки́ра:** О, Том! Приве́т! Как дела́?
> **Том:** Приве́т, Ки́ра! Норма́льно. А у тебя́?
> **Ки́ра:** То́же норма́льно. Том, э́то Ками́ла. Ками́ла, э́то Том.
> **Том:** Приве́т! Моё и́мя Том.
> **Ками́ла:** Ками́ла. О́чень прия́тно!
> **Ки́ра:** Ко́фе и́ли чай?
> **Ками́ла:** Ко́фе, пожа́луйста!
> **Том:** Чай, пожа́луйста. И… шокола́д?
> **Ки́ра:** И шокола́д.

*An hour later, at the door of Kira's flat.*

> **Том:** Спаси́бо, Ки́ра! Пока́!
> **Ками́ла:** Спаси́бо! До встре́чи!
> **Ки́ра:** Споко́йной но́чи!

*That night, in Camila's flat, still full of boxes. There's a knock at the door.*

> **Ками́ла:** Кто там?
> **…:** Мя́у!
> **Ками́ла:** Ба́рсик? Приве́т!

Read it twice: the first time straight through, even if you don't get
everything; the second time slowly, with the words below.

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

The whole episode comes from the unit's two weeks except these five, which
explain themselves:

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ой! | oh!, oops! | Ой! Кот! |
| Ничего́! | no problem!, it's fine! | — Извини́те! — Ничего́! |
| А ты? | and you? | Я Ки́ра. А ты? |
| моё и́мя | my name | Моё и́мя Том. |
| мя́у | miaow | — Кто там? — Мя́у! |

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Barsik is Kira's cat.
2. Kira and Tom already knew each other.
3. Tom asks for coffee.
4. Camila says goodbye with **До встре́чи**.
5. That night, Kira knocks at Camila's door.

### Б. Найди́те две оши́бки.

*Camila says "Я есть Ками́ла" and Tom says "Моё и́мя Том". One is the typical mistake of a Spanish speaker, the other that of an English speaker. Which one is yours? How would a Russian say it?*

### В. Чита́йте по ро́лям.

*Read the episode aloud doing all three voices, with the lines corrected. Record yourself and listen: do your questions jump where they should?*

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** who Camila, Tom, Kira and Barsik are, and you've read a whole episode in Russian with what you learnt in two weeks.

Unit 2 starts tomorrow: reading Russian for real, with all its rules.

## Answers

**А.** 1 true · 2 true: Kira asks him **Как дела́?**, and you only ask that
of people you know · 3 false: he asks for tea, and chocolate · 4 true · 5
false: it's Barsik.

**Б.** Yours is Tom's: **Моё и́мя Том** is "my name is Tom" word for word. It's
understood, but it sounds like filling in a form; a Russian would say
**Меня́ зову́т Том**. Camila's is the Spanish speaker's: **Я есть Ками́ла**
adds the "am" Russian leaves out. A Russian would say **Я Ками́ла** or
**Меня́ зову́т Ками́ла**.

**The episode in English.** — Oh! A cat! Who's that? — Barsik! Barsik, no!
Sorry! — It's fine! — I'm Kira. And you? — I'm Camila. — Camila? Nice to meet
you! · — Oh, Tom! Hi! How are things? — Hi, Kira! Fine. And you? — Fine too.
Tom, this is Camila. Camila, this is Tom. — Hi! My name is Tom. — Camila.
Nice to meet you! — Coffee or tea? — Coffee, please! — Tea, please. And…
chocolate? — And chocolate. · — Thanks, Kira! Bye! — Thanks! See you! — Good
night! · — Who's there? — Miaow! — Barsik? Hi!
