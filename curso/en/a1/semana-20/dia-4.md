> **Today there's no new grammar: there's a combination.** You join a unit 9 verb with a unit 10 location in the same sentence. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - combine **Где?** and **Что де́лаешь?** into one answer;
> - say what each character does and where;
> - ask about the place of an action, not just the place of a person.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's rule, from memory:

> в [f] ко́мнате · в [v] до́ме

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Чита́ет** and **рису́ет** sound the same as always; what's new
today is the whole sentence, not any single word.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> Том чита́ет в гости́ной. Ми́ша рису́ет в ко́мнате.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**You can combine a verb with a place in the same sentence, with no
connector: the verb first, the place after.** The two questions you
already know, **Где?** and **Что де́лаешь?**, can be answered at the
same time:

> Я гото́влю на ку́хне.
> *I cook in the kitchen.*
> Том чита́ет в гости́ной.
> *Tom reads in the living room.*
> Ки́ра спит в спа́льне.
> *Kira sleeps in the bedroom.*
> Ми́ша рису́ет в ко́мнате.
> *Misha draws in the room.*

**And you can ask in two different ways**, depending on what you
want to know:

> — Что ты де́лаешь на ку́хне?
> *What do you do in the kitchen?* (you know the place, you ask the action)
> — Где Том чита́ет?
> *Where does Tom read?* (you know the action, you ask the place)

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also joins the verb and the place with no connector — "I
read in the living room"— so word order isn't the trap here.** The
trap is remembering to give the place its correct ending, which is
easy to forget when the sentence already has a verb holding your
attention:

| English (the place doesn't change) | Russian (the place DOES change) |
|---|---|
| I read IN the living room | чита́ю **в гости́ной** |

It's not enough to remember в or на: the noun's ending has to
change too, every time.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я гото́влю на ку́хне. Том чита́ет в гости́ной. Ки́ра спит в спа́льне.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. Misha draws in the room.
2. What do you do in the kitchen?
3. Where does Tom read?

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ками́ла пи́шет в спа́льне.
2. Где ты поёшь?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Four combinations.** Say out loud what you do and where, using a
unit 9 verb and a unit 10 place in each sentence.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** how to combine a verb with a place in one sentence, with no connector, and how to ask about the action or the place depending on what I need to know.
