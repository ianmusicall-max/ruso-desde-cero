> **Today there are no new words: there's a sound rule.** By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read **в** linked to the next word, with no pause;
> - know when **в** sounds [f] and when it sounds [v];
> - apply the rule to the unit's six location chunks.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's adjective, from memory:

> люби́мый · люби́мая · люби́мое · люби́мые

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**В is never pronounced on its own: it attaches to the next word,
as if it were its first letter.** And if that word starts with a
VOICELESS consonant (к, с, т, п, ф, ш), **в** devoices and sounds
[f].

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> в ко́мнате [f-KOM-na-tie] — в кварти́ре [f-kvar-TEE-rie] —
> в спа́льне [f-SPAL'-nie]

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**The rule is about the SOUND that comes after в, not the whole
word.** Compare the six location chunks you already know:

| Chunk | First sound | в sounds |
|---|---|---|
| в ко́мнате | к (voiceless) | [f] |
| в кварти́ре | к (voiceless) | [f] |
| в спа́льне | с (voiceless) | [f] |
| в коридо́ре | к (voiceless) | [f] |
| в до́ме | д (voiced) | [v] |
| в гости́ной | г (voiced) | [v] |

> В ко́мнате ти́хо. [f-KOM-na-tie]
> *It's quiet in the room.*
> В до́ме гро́мко. [v-DO-mie]
> *It's loud in the house.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English has no such rule for its prepositions: "in" sounds the
same before any word. Russian does, and it affects almost every
one-letter preposition, not just в.**

| English (no change) | Russian (в changes with what follows) |
|---|---|
| IN the room, IN the house | **в** [f] ко́мнате, **в** [v] до́ме |

You don't need to memorize which consonant is voiceless and which
is voiced for every new word: just listen to the first consonant
and apply the rule, and in time it comes out on its own.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, linking в to the word and marking whether it sounds [f] or [v].*

> в ко́мнате — в до́ме — в кварти́ре — в гости́ной — в коридо́ре —
> в спа́льне

### Б. [f] и́ли [v]?

*Decide how в sounds in each chunk.*

1. в ку́хне
2. в до́ме
3. в шкафу́
4. в гости́ной

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. В ко́мнате ти́хо.
2. В до́ме гро́мко.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**The six chunks.** Read them aloud one after another, linking **в**
each time and deciding whether it sounds [f] or [v] before you say
it.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** **в** attaches to the next word and devoices to [f] before a voiceless consonant; before a voiced one, it sounds [v].

## Today's vocabulary

*There are no new words: today is a sound rule about the location
chunks you already know.*

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ти́хо | it's quiet | В ко́мнате ти́хо. |
| гро́мко | it's loud | В до́ме гро́мко. |
| в ко́мнате [f] | in the room | В ко́мнате ти́хо. |
| в до́ме [v] | at home | В до́ме гро́мко. |
| в кварти́ре [f] | in the apartment | В кварти́ре ти́хо. |

## Answers

**Б.** 1 [f] (к is voiceless) · 2 [v] (д is voiced) · 3 [f] (ш is
voiceless) · 4 [v] (г is voiced)

**В.** 1 It's quiet in the room. · 2 It's loud in the house.
