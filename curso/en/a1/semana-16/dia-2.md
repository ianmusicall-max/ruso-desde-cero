> **Today there are no new words: there's a scale.** You rank three degrees you already know separately, from most to least. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - say **о́чень люблю́**, stronger than a plain **люблю́**;
> - rank three levels of liking, from most to least;
> - keep the direct object the same across all three.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The verb and the adverb, from memory:

> люблю́ · о́чень

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**О́чень люблю́** are two words, each with its own stress, kept apart:
they don't blend into one.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> о́чень люблю́ — люблю́ — не люблю́

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Three degrees, from most to least:**

> о́чень люблю́ > люблю́ > не люблю́

**О́чень** you already know from the "Как дела́?" scale —**о́чень
хорошо́**, **о́чень пло́хо**—; today it joins **люблю́** to raise it a
degree. The direct object stays the same as always, at all three
levels:

> — Ты лю́бишь кино́?
> *Do you like the movies?*
> — Я о́чень люблю́ кино́!
> *I love the movies!*
> — А джаз?
> *And jazz?*
> — Люблю́ джаз, но не люблю́ рок.
> *I like jazz, but I don't like rock.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also grades "like," but with different words at each step:
"I love it," "I like it," "I don't like it." Russian uses the SAME
word, люблю́, and raises or lowers the degree with о́чень or не.**

| English (three words) | Russian (one word, two markers) |
|---|---|
| I love it / I like it / I don't like it | о́чень люблю́ / люблю́ / не люблю́ |

Don't look for a separate verb for "love": in Russian it's the same
**люби́ть**, just stronger with **о́чень** in front.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, with the intonation that matches each degree.*

> Я о́чень люблю́ кино́. · Я люблю́ джаз. · Я не люблю́ рок.

### Б. Rank from most to least.

*Number 1 (most) to 3 (least).*

1. Я люблю́ пи́ццу.
2. Я о́чень люблю́ пи́ццу.
3. Я не люблю́ пи́ццу.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Я о́чень люблю́ му́зыку.
2. Я люблю́ футбо́л, но не люблю́ бале́т.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Three degrees, three hobbies.** Pick three of this week's hobbies and
say, out loud, one with **о́чень люблю́**, another with **люблю́** and
another with **не люблю́**.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** the three degrees of **люби́ть** —о́чень люблю́, люблю́, не люблю́— using the same word every time, with the direct object unchanged.
