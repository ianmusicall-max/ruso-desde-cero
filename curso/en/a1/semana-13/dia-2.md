> **Today** you learn that "my" changes shape depending on the noun it goes with. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - choose between **мой, моя́, моё** and **мои́** without hesitating;
> - use **мои́** even when you're talking about a single clock;
> - recognize **ключи́**, the word for "keys."

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's genders, out loud:

> мой брат — он · моя́ сестра́ — она́ · моё письмо́ — оно́

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Мои́** doesn't sound like **мой**: the final **и** isn't the same
sound as **й**.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> мой — моя́ — моё — мои́
> мои́ ключи́

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**You've been using мой for weeks without thinking about it.** Today
you find out it isn't one word: it's four, and the noun decides which.

| Word | Used with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| мой | masculine | мой стол |
| моя́ | feminine | моя́ ла́мпа |
| моё | neuter | моё письмо́ |
| мои́ | plural | мои́ ключи́ |

The rule is the same one you already know from **он/она́/оно́/они́**: the
object's gender decides, not yours.

**Ключи́** is the word for "keys" in the plural: learn it whole, like
**часы́**, **очки́** and **де́ньги** —a plural you simply memorize—; the
full rule for plurals comes in unit 12.

> — Где мой брат?
> *Where's my brother?*
> — Он здесь. А где моя́ сестра́?
> *He's here. And where's my sister?*
> — Она́ там. А э́то мои́ ключи́?
> *She's there. And are these my keys?*
> — Да, твои́.
> *Yes, they're yours.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English has one word for "my"; Russian has four.** In English, "my
table" and "my clock" use the same word; in Russian, both change:

| English (one form) | Russian (four forms) |
|---|---|
| my table, my clock | моя́ ла́мпа *(feminine)*, мои́ часы́ *(plural)* |
| my brother, my sister | мой брат *(masculine)*, моя́ сестра́ *(feminine)* |

There's no "safe" single form: every noun asks for its own, the same way
you already decide which pronoun to use.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> мой стол · моя́ сестра́ · моё письмо́ · мои́ ключи́

### Б. Мой, моя́, моё и́ли мои́?

*Choose the correct form.*

1. ______ брат (masculine)
2. ______ сестра́ (feminine)
3. ______ письмо́ (neuter)
4. ______ ключи́ (plural)
5. ______ ба́бушка (feminine)

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Моя́ ла́мпа здесь.
2. Где мои́ ключи́?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Six things of yours.** Name six objects or people you already know
—family or household items— and put the correct form of "my" in front
of each one, out loud.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> мой брат · моя́ сестра́ · моё письмо́ · мои́ ключи́
>
> **Now I know:** "my" has four forms, and the object's gender decides which one.

Tomorrow: the same rule with **твой** and **ваш**.

## Answers

**Б.** 1 Мой · 2 Моя́ · 3 Моё · 4 Мои́ · 5 Моя́

**В.** 1 My lamp is here. · 2 Where are my keys?
