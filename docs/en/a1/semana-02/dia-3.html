A1 · Unit 1 · Week 2 · Day 3 · Lesson

Ж, Ш, Ч, Щ, Ц. Coffee or tea?

Ж, Ш, Ч, Щ, Ц. Ко́фе и́ли чай?

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you learn five consonants, three of which English writes with two letters, and how to offer someone a drink. By the end you'll be able to:

  • read Ж, Ш, Ч, Щ, Ц;
  • ask Ко́фе и́ли чай? and answer Чай, пожа́луйста;
  • tell ко́фе from кафе́.

1Warm-up0–3 min

Answer aloud, without looking back at yesterday:

Someone hands you a yoghurt: … · Someone thanks you: … · You step on someone's foot: …

(Спаси́бо! · Пожа́луйста. · Извини́те!)

2Pronunciation3–8 min

Letter Sounds Hint
Ж ж [zh] the s in measure; always hard
Ш ш [sh] sh, with the tongue pulled back; always hard
Ч ч [ch] ch in cheese, softer; always soft
Щ щ long soft [sh] the sh sh of fresh sheep, lips in a smile
Ц ц [ts] the ts of cats, in one go

Слу́шайте и повторя́йте. Listen and repeat:

жа — жу — же · ша — шу — шо · ча — чу — чи
ща — щу — щи · ца — цу — цо

Ш and ж are a pair, like с and з: the same tongue position, without and with voice. Say ш with the tip of your tongue a little further back than for English sh, lips slightly rounded; now put your hand on your throat and make the same sound buzz: ж.

3New8–18 min

With 27 letters, a café menu:

Word Say it Means
ко́фе KO-fye coffee
чай chai tea
и́ли EE-li or
журна́л zhoor-NAL magazine
шокола́д sha-ka-LAT chocolate
цирк tsirk circus
борщ borshch borscht, beetroot soup
пи́цца PEE-tsa pizza

Look at шокола́д: the unstressed о's sound [a], twice, and the final д sounds almost like a t. The golden rule works everywhere.

And now you can read all of что. It's still said [shto]: an exception.

— Ко́фе и́ли чай?
Coffee or tea?
— Чай, пожа́луйста.
Tea, please.
— Вот чай. И шокола́д!
Here's your tea. And chocolate!
— Спаси́бо!
Thanks!

In a question with и́ли the voice goes up on the first choice and down on the second, just like in English: Ко́↗фе и́ли ча↘й?

⚠️ Contrast with English

Ts at the start of a word. English has [ts] only at the end (cats, hits). Russian puts it anywhere, starting words too: цирк is [tsirk], not "sirk" or "kirk". Say cats, then cut off the ca: ts… — and go straight on: ц-ирк.

And two kinds of sh. English has one; Russian has a hard ш (tongue back, rather dark) and a soft, long щ (tongue up, lips smiling):

Word Sound
ша́пка hard ш, dark
щи soft щ, long: fresh sheep
борщ soft щ at the end

4Practice18–25 min

А. Чита́йте.

Read aloud. At the end: which is the drink and which is the place?

чай — журна́л — шокола́д — цирк — борщ — пи́цца — ша́пка — щи — ко́фе — кафе́

Б. Ко́фе и́ли чай?

Answer each question by choosing one of the two, with пожа́луйста.

  1. Ко́фе и́ли чай?
  2. Сок и́ли вода́?
  3. Пи́цца и́ли борщ?
  4. Йо́гурт и́ли шокола́д?
  5. Футбо́л и́ли цирк?

В. Ж, Ш, Ч, Щ и́ли Ц?

Write the missing letter.

  1. …ай
  2. …урна́л
  3. …окола́д
  4. …ирк
  5. бор…
  6. …а́пка (hat)

5Your turn25–29 min

You've got guests. Offer each one a drink and answer for them. Watch your voice: up on the first choice, down on the second.

— Ко́фе и́ли чай? — Ко́фе, пожа́луйста. — Вот ко́фе. — Спаси́бо!
— Сок и́ли вода́? — Вода́, пожа́луйста. — Пожа́луйста!

Record yourself. Then write the five letters by hand, capital and small: щ and ц have a little tail at the bottom.

6Wrap-up29–30 min

I can now say:
Ко́фе и́ли чай? · Чай, пожа́луйста. · Вот кафе́.

I know: 27 of 33 letters. 6 to go.

Tomorrow: the vowels with a y in front, and ы, a vowel English doesn't have.

Show the answers

А. Ко́фе is the drink and кафе́ the place. Ша́пка is a hat and щи a cabbage soup.

Б. Either one, with пожа́луйста: "Чай, пожа́луйста", "Вода́, пожа́луйста". What doesn't work is "Да": it isn't a yes/no question.

В. 1 ч (чай) · 2 ж (журна́л) · 3 ш (шокола́д) · 4 ц (цирк) · 5 щ (борщ) · 6 ш (ша́пка)
