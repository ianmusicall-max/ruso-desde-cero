**The traps.** In 6 and 8, the wrong answer almost always copies the
English ending pattern ("рисова́ю," "писа́ешь"): the `-ова-`/`-ева-` and
писа́ть's с ALWAYS disappear in the present, with no form keeping them. In
9, гото́вить adds an л in "я," just like люби́ть, but —unlike
люби́ть— it never shifts the stress in any other form: that's the
difference that separates the two families. In 12, ша́хматы is plural, so
it requires нра́вятся even though "you" doesn't change: нра́вится/нра́вятся
doesn't conjugate by who likes it, but by what's liked.
