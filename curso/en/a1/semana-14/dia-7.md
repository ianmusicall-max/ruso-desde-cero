> **Today** Camila, Tom and Kira show each other photos of their families in the courtyard, and at the end they exchange phone numbers. By the end you'll have read a full unit-7 scene without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**Showing photos on your phone is like introducing your family without
them being there.** It's something that happens between new neighbors,
without anyone asking for it: Camila has been in the building a few
weeks, and this is the first time Tom and Kira see where she comes
from.

Before you read, think: if you showed a new neighbor a photo of your
family, who would you start with?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Эпизо́д 7. Фо́то на телефо́не

*Saturday afternoon, in the courtyard. Camila is showing photos on her
phone.*

> **Ками́ла:** Э́то моя́ семья́. Вот ма́ма, а вот па́па. Мои́ роди́тели.
> **Том:** А кто э́то, ря́дом?
> **Ками́ла:** Э́то мой брат. Он из Перу́, как я.
> **Том:** А э́то твоя́ сестра́?
> **Ками́ла:** Нет, э́то не сестра́. Э́то моя́ тётя.

*Tom shows his own photos, from Toronto.*

> **Том:** А вот моя́ семья́. Э́то мой брат, а э́то моя́ ба́бушка.
> **Ками́ла:** А где твои́ роди́тели?
> **Том:** Вот они́. А э́то наш дом, в Торо́нто.

*Kira arrives.*

> **Ки́ра:** Приве́т! Чья э́то фотогра́фия?
> **Ками́ла:** Моя́. А где твоя́ ма́ма, Ки́ра?
> **Ки́ра:** Она́ из Каза́ни. Э́то её дом.

*Before leaving, they exchange phone numbers.*

> **Том:** Ки́ра, у вас есть телефо́н?
> **Ки́ра:** Да, есть. Мой но́мер: во́семь, два, семь, четы́ре.
> **Том:** Повтори́те, пожа́луйста.
> **Ки́ра:** Во́семь, два, семь, четы́ре.
> **Том:** Спаси́бо!
> **Ками́ла:** А твой но́мер, Том?
> **Том:** Шесть, оди́н, во́семь, три. Э́то мой но́мер.

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**No new words today**: the whole episode comes from units 1 to 7. The
only thing that extends is a rule you already know:

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Она́ из Каза́ни. | She's from Kazán. | Она́ из Каза́ни. |

**Из + genitive** you already used with countries (**из Перу́**, **из
Кана́ды**); today the same rule works just the same with a city.

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Camila's brother is from Peru.
2. The person next to Camila's mom is her sister.
3. Kira's mother is from Kazán.
4. At the end, nobody exchanges phone numbers.

### Б. Найди́те оши́бку.

*Tom asks "Is this your sister?" and Camila says no. Who is the person
in the photo, really? Why does Tom think she's a sister?*

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Она́ из Каза́ни.
2. Чья э́то фотогра́фия?

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** how to show and ask about family in a photo, and that **из + genitive** works just as well for a city as it does for a country.
>
> This closes unit 7. Next week, unit 8.

## Answers

**А.** 1 true · 2 false: she's his aunt · 3 true · 4 false: all three
exchange phone numbers at the end

**Б.** She's his aunt, not his sister. Tom assumes it because, seeing
two young-looking people together in a photo, he jumps to "siblings":
how close in age two people look says nothing about how they're
actually related.

**В.** 1 She's from Kazán. · 2 Whose photo is this?
