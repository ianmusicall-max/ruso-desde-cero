> **Today** you find out why **меня́** sounds [mi-NYA] and not [me-NYA]. It's the course's second reduction rule, and the one that changes the most words. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read any unstressed **е** and any unstressed **я**;
> - say six long job names without tripping;
> - explain why **тебя́** isn't read the way it's written.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's six forms, from memory and without moving the stress:

> рабо́таю — рабо́таешь — рабо́тает — рабо́таем — рабо́таете — рабо́тают

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

You already know what happens to **о**: unstressed, it sounds [a]. Well,
**е** and **я** do the same thing, but they close towards **и**:

| Letter | Stressed | Unstressed |
|---|---|---|
| е | [e] — **ме́сто** [MYES-ta] | [i] — **меня́** [mi-NYA] |
| я | [ya] — **я́блоко** [YA-bla-ka] | [i] — **язы́к** [yi-ZYK] |

In other words: **the only vowel that keeps its full value is the stressed
one**. Everything else shrinks.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat. The strong syllable first, then the whole word:*

> ня́ — меня́ · бя́ — тебя́ · зы́к — язы́к
> та́рь — секрета́рь · стра́ — медсестра́

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Three words you were already saying right without knowing why:**

| Word | ✗ As it's written | ✓ As it's said |
|---|---|---|
| меня́ | ~~me-nya~~ | mi-**NYA** |
| тебя́ | ~~te-bya~~ | ti-**BYA** |
| язы́к | ~~ya-zyk~~ | yi-**ZYK** |

You picked them up by ear in units 3 and 4. Today you have the rule behind
them, and with it you can read aloud a word you've never heard.

**And six jobs to practise on:**

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| секрета́рь | si-kri-**TAR'** | secretary |
| медсестра́ | mit-sis-**TRA** | nurse |
| перево́дчик | pi-ri-**VOT**-chik | translator, interpreter |
| продаве́ц | pra-da-**VYETS** | shop assistant, salesperson |
| программи́ст | pra-gra-**MEEST** | programmer |
| профе́ссия | pra-**FYE**-si-ya | profession |

Look at **продаве́ц**: there the **е** does carry the stress, so it sounds
[e] and not [i]. The rule isn't "е sounds [i]"; it's "**unstressed** е sounds
[i]".

**And two bits of catching you already know.** In **медсестра́** the **д**
comes before **с**, so it goes voiceless: [mit-]. That's the unit 4 rule at
work inside a new word.

> — Кем вы рабо́таете?
> *What do you do for a living?*
> — Я перево́дчик. А вы?
> *I'm a translator. And you?*
> — Я медсестра́.
> *I'm a nurse.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English shrinks its unstressed vowels too — into the wrong sound.** In
*banana* the two unstressed a's go to a schwa, that flat uh. Russian doesn't
have that: its unstressed е and я go to [i], a clear front vowel. So the
habit transfers and the target doesn't.

| Word | ✗ With an English schwa | ✓ With a Russian [i] |
|---|---|---|
| меня́ | ~~[mə-NYA]~~ | [mi-NYA] |
| секрета́рь | ~~[sə-krə-TAR']~~ | [si-kri-TAR'] |
| перево́дчик | ~~[pə-rə-VOT-chik]~~ | [pi-ri-VOT-chik] |

**The trick that works:** say the word **with the strong syllable alone**
first, then add the rest at full speed, giving them no time to turn into
schwa. First [TAR'], then [kri-TAR'], then [si-kri-TAR'].

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud. Find the stressed syllable first, then shrink all the others.*

> секрета́рь · медсестра́ · перево́дчик · продаве́ц · программи́ст
> профе́ссия · меня́ · тебя́ · язы́к

### Б. Как говори́м?

*Choose the right pronunciation.*

1. тебя́ → а) [te-bya] · б) [ti-bya]
2. продаве́ц → а) [pra-da-vyets] · б) [pra-da-vits]
3. язы́к → а) [ya-zyk] · б) [yi-zyk]
4. секрета́рь → а) [si-kri-tar'] · б) [se-kre-tar']
5. профе́ссия → а) [pra-fye-si-ya] · б) [pro-fe-si-ya]

### В. Где ударе́ние?

*Which syllable takes the stress? Say it, then check above.*

1. медсестра́
2. программи́ст
3. перево́дчик
4. профе́ссия

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Hunt the unstressed ones.** Take ten Russian words you already know with
**е** or **я** in them and read them aloud, finding the strong syllable
first:

> меня́ · тебя́ · язы́к · перево́дчик · де́вять · де́сять

Then **record yourself** reading the list of six jobs and listen: does any
unstressed **е** come out as a schwa? If one does, repeat that word five
times starting from the strong syllable on its own.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> секрета́рь, медсестра́, перево́дчик, продаве́ц, программи́ст, профе́ссия
>
> **I know:** unstressed **е** and **я** close into [i]. Only the strong syllable keeps its whole vowel.
