> **Today** you pull the whole unit together: you describe the place you live in, in the order it's told. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - describe your city in five sentences;
> - place each spot with **в це́нтре**, **спра́ва**, **сле́ва** and **там**;
> - say which bus is yours.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's nine words, aloud and with the catching:

> авто́бус · ло́жка · ска́зка · вокза́л · футбо́л · ло́дка
> сде́лать · ю́бка · остано́вка

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Today's words are long, and that shows in the unstressed vowels.** Find
the stress and let the rest fall:

| Word | Say it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| го́род | **GO**-rat | the final о sounds [a] and the final д goes quiet |
| ры́нок | **RÝ**-nak | the unstressed о sounds [a] |
| магази́н | ma-ga-**ZEEN** | both unstressed а vowels weaken |
| стадио́н | sta-di-**ON** | stress at the end, like авто́бус |

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat, with the stress in the right place:*

> го́род [GO-rat] · ры́нок [RÝ-nak] · магази́н [ma-ga-ZEEN]
> река́ [ri-KA] · мост [most] · стадио́н [sta-di-ON] · кварти́ра [kvar-TEE-ra]

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Describing a place is an order, not a list.** First you say what it is,
then what's in the middle, then the sides, and last what's far away:

| | What you say | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | what it is | Э́то мой го́род. |
| 2 | the centre | В це́нтре пло́щадь. |
| 3 | the right | Спра́ва теа́тр. |
| 4 | the left | Сле́ва ры́нок. |
| 5 | what's far | Там река́ и мост. |

**Look at what isn't there**: no "is", no "there's", no "a", no "the".
**Спра́ва теа́тр** is a whole sentence, and it means "there's a theatre on
the right".

> — Э́то твой го́род?
> *Is this your city?*
> — Да, э́то мой го́род. В це́нтре пло́щадь, спра́ва вокза́л.
> *Yes, this is my city. The square is in the centre, the station on the right.*
> — А река́?
> *And the river?*
> — Река́ далеко́. Там мост.
> *The river is far away. The bridge is over there.*

**And numbers tell you which one.** In Russian the number goes after, as in
English:

> авто́бус семь · трамва́й два · авто́бус двена́дцать

And to answer **Ско́лько?** the bare number is enough:

> — Ско́лько? — Три.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English gives you nothing to lean on here: it has no gender.** "My" is
"my" for everything, so **мой** feels like one word — and it isn't. Which
form you use depends on the ending of the Russian noun:

| You mean | ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|---|
| my city | ~~моя́ го́род~~ | **мой го́род** (го́род ends in a consonant) |
| my mum | ~~мой ма́ма~~ | моя́ ма́ма (ма́ма ends in -а) |

That's why you learnt gender on day 8: it is the thing that decides. For
now take **мой го́род** away as a block; the full possessives come in
unit 7.

**And one word that will trick you: магази́н is not a magazine**, it's a
**shop**. A magazine is **журна́л**.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, with the unstressed vowels weak.*

> Э́то мой го́род. В це́нтре пло́щадь. Спра́ва магази́н и апте́ка.
> Сле́ва шко́ла. Там река́, мост и стадио́н.

### Б. Где?

*Place each spot with the word you're given.*

> ры́нок (спра́ва) → Спра́ва ры́нок.

1. банк (сле́ва)
2. гости́ница (в це́нтре)
3. вокза́л (далеко́)
4. остано́вка (ря́дом)
5. стадио́н (там)

### В. Како́й авто́бус?

*Say aloud which bus or tram it is.*

1. авто́бус 5
2. трамва́й 11
3. авто́бус 20
4. трамва́й 8

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your city in five sentences.** Write the five from the order above about
the place you actually live, then say them while recording yourself:

> Э́то мой го́род. В це́нтре… Спра́ва… Сле́ва… Там…

Listen: does **го́род** end in a [t]? Does **магази́н** come out with the
stress on the last syllable?

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> мой го́род, магази́н, ры́нок, река́, мост, стадио́н, кварти́ра
>
> **I know:** describing a place is an order —what it is, the centre, the sides, what's far—, that Russian says it with no "is" and no "there's", and that **мой** changes with the noun's ending.
