> **Today** you start the house unit with a simple floor plan. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - name six parts of a home;
> - use **спа́льня** and **гости́ная**, the two new ones;
> - get ready to say WHERE something is, which comes tomorrow.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Last week's four verbs, from memory:

> пишу́ · гото́влю · пою́ · сплю́

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Спа́льня** and **гости́ная** are stressed where the mark shows;
neither is hard to read if you go syllable by syllable.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> спа́льня — гости́ная

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**To name a room, Э́то + the word, in its dictionary form, is
enough**, just like you already did with "Э́то кот" in the first
week. Today you only name things; tomorrow you'll learn to say
WHERE someone is.

| Russian | Sounds like | Means |
|---|---|---|
| дом | dom | house |
| кварти́ра | kvar-TEE-ra | apartment, flat |
| ко́мната | KOM-na-ta | room (generic) |
| ку́хня | KOOJ-nya | kitchen |
| спа́льня | SPAL'-nya | bedroom |
| гости́ная | gas-TEE-na-ya | living room |

> Э́то дом. Э́то кварти́ра. Э́то ко́мната.
> Э́то ку́хня. Э́то спа́льня. Э́то гости́ная.

**Дом** is the whole house, outside or in; **кварти́ра** is a flat
inside a building; **ко́мната** is any room with no specific name,
and the other three —ку́хня, спа́льня, гости́ная— are rooms with their
own name.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also distinguishes "house" from "flat" and "room" from
"kitchen"; the difference is that Russian uses no article in front
of any of them.**

| English (with an article) | Russian (no article) |
|---|---|
| This is THE kitchen. | Э́то ку́хня. |
| This is THE bedroom. | Э́то спа́льня. |

You already know this from the first week: Russian has no
articles, no "the/a." This unit doesn't change that rule.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Э́то кварти́ра. Э́то ку́хня. Э́то спа́льня. Э́то гости́ная.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. This is an apartment.
2. This is the kitchen.
3. This is the living room.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Э́то ко́мната.
2. Э́то дом, а э́то кварти́ра.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your floor plan.** Say today's six words out loud, pointing to (or
imagining) each part of your own home.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Э́то дом. · Э́то кварти́ра. · Э́то спа́льня. · Э́то гости́ная.
>
> **Now I know:** six words for the parts of a home, with no article in front of any of them.

Tomorrow: saying where someone is.
