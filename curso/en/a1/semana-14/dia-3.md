> **Today** you ask for and give an email address. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - ask **Како́й у вас име́йл?**;
> - read **@** as **соба́ка** and the dot as **то́чка**;
> - spell out a whole address in Russian.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The Latin alphabet you already use for writing, and last week's
digits, out loud:

> оди́н — два — три — четы́ре

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Соба́ка** and **то́чка** are ordinary Russian words —"dog" and
"dot/point"— that switch jobs here.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> како́й — соба́ка — то́чка
> Како́й у вас име́йл?

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Since you already know Како́й у вас но́мер?, today you learn the same
question for a different piece of information.**

| Word | It sounds like | It means |
|---|---|---|
| како́й | ka-**KOY** | which? |
| име́йл | i-**MEYL** | email (the piece of information) |
| соба́ка | sa-**BA**-ka | "dog"; here, the **@** sign |
| то́чка | **TOCH**-ka | "dot/point"; here, the **.** sign |

When spelling out an address, **@** is read **соба́ка** and the dot,
**то́чка**: nobody says "at," and nobody skips it in silence.

> — Како́й у вас име́йл?
> *What's your email?*
> — Tom соба́ка example то́чка com.
> *Tom dog example dot com.*
> — Повтори́те, пожа́луйста.
> *Repeat that, please.*
> — Tom соба́ка example то́чка com.
> — Пра́вильно?
> *Right?*
> — Да, пра́вильно.

**The Latin letters in the address are said as letters**, one at a time
if needed; the only things that switch languages are **соба́ка** and
**то́чка**.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English reads @ as "at"; Russian reads it as "соба́ка" (dog), after
the shape of its tail.** Two completely different images for the same
sign:

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| name at domain dot com | и́мя соба́ка до́мен то́чка ком |

Say "at" in Russian and you won't be understood: you need to swap the
whole image, not just the word.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, with соба́ка and то́чка in their place.*

> Tom соба́ка example то́чка com

### Б. Which sign is it?

*Choose the matching sign.*

1. соба́ка → а) @ · б) . · в) #
2. то́чка → а) @ · б) . · в) #

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Како́й у вас име́йл?
2. Повтори́те, пожа́луйста.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Spell out your email.** Spell out an email address out loud —your
own, or one you make up— with **соба́ка** and **то́чка** in their place.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Како́й у вас име́йл? · [name] соба́ка [domain] то́чка com
>
> **Now I know:** that @ is read **соба́ка** and the dot, **то́чка**, and those two words only switch jobs in this one context.
