> **Today** you learn three letters and the words of good manners. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read **Ф, З, Й**;
> - say thank you and reply to a thank you: **Спаси́бо. — Пожа́луйста.**
> - say sorry: **Извини́те!**

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Ask and answer aloud, both voices:

> — Приве́т! Как дела́?
> — Хорошо́, спаси́бо. А у тебя́?
> — То́же хорошо́.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

| Letter | Sounds | As in |
|---|---|---|
| Ф ф | [f] | **f**oto |
| З з | [z] | **z**oo |
| Й й | a short [y] | bo**y**, ma**y** |

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> фа — фо — фу · за — зо — зу · ай — ой — уй — эй

Good news: all three sounds exist in English. **Й** is the short *y* that
closes a syllable, as in *boy*: **май** (May) is one syllable, like *my*.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

With 22 letters you can read nearly everything you'll see on a street:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| фо́то | **FO**-ta | photo |
| футбо́л | food-**BOL** | football, soccer |
| газе́та | ga-**ZYE**-ta | newspaper |
| музе́й | moo-**ZYEY** | museum |
| зоопа́рк | za-a-**PARK** | zoo |
| йо́гурт | **YO**-goort | yoghurt |
| кафе́ | ka-**FEH** | café |

Notice the **т** in **футбо́л**: before **б** it sounds almost like a d. It's
the rule of consonants that rub off on each other, and unit 4 explains it.

And the three words of good manners:

| Phrase | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Спаси́бо. | spa-**SEE**-ba | Thank you. |
| Пожа́луйста. | pa-**ZHAL**-sta | Please. / You're welcome. / Here you are. |
| Извини́те! | iz-vi-**NEE**-tye | Excuse me! Sorry! |

**Пожа́луйста** is written with more letters than you hear: the **у** and
the **й** almost vanish. Learn it by ear: [pa-ZHAL-sta].

> — Йо́гурт, пожа́луйста.
> *A yoghurt, please.*
> — Пожа́луйста.
> *Here you are.*
> — Спаси́бо!
> *Thanks!*
> — Пожа́луйста!
> *You're welcome!*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**One word for three English phrases.** Russian **пожа́луйста** asks,
gives and answers a thank you:

| In English | In Russian |
|---|---|
| A coffee, **please**. | Ко́фе, **пожа́луйста**. |
| **Here you are.** | **Пожа́луйста.** |
| — Thanks. — **You're welcome.** | — Спаси́бо. — **Пожа́луйста.** |

**And familiar words, Russian stress:** *FOOTball* is **футбо́л**
[food-**BOL**].

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> фо́то — футбо́л — газе́та — музе́й — зоопа́рк — йо́гурт — кафе́ — коза́ — коса́ — зе́бра — май

### Б. Спаси́бо, пожа́луйста и́ли извини́те?

*What do you say?*

1. Someone hands you a coffee.
2. Someone thanks you.
3. You step on someone's foot on the metro.
4. You order a yoghurt.
5. You hand someone a photo.

### В. З и́ли с?

*Write з or с.*

1. …оопа́рк
2. му…е́й
3. …ок
4. ко…а́ (goat)
5. ко…а́ (plait)
6. спа…и́бо

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**At the café.** Act out the scene, both voices: you order, they hand it
over, you say thanks, they reply. And on the way out you bump into someone:

> — Йо́гурт и ко́фе, пожа́луйста.
> — Пожа́луйста.
> — Спаси́бо!
> — Пожа́луйста!
> — Ой… Извини́те!

Do it again ordering something else: **сок**, **торт**, **вода́**. Then
write **Ф, З, Й** by hand, capital and small.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> Спаси́бо. — Пожа́луйста. · Йо́гурт, пожа́луйста. · Извини́те!
>
> **I know:** 22 of 33 letters. 11 to go.

Tomorrow: five consonants, three of which English writes with two letters,
and the big afternoon question: coffee or tea?

## Answers

**А.** **Зе́бра** is "zebra" and **май** is "May". **Коза́** (goat) and
**коса́** (plait) differ only in the з.

**Б.** 1 Спаси́бо! · 2 Пожа́луйста. · 3 Извини́те! · 4 Йо́гурт,
пожа́луйста. · 5 Пожа́луйста.

**В.** 1 з (зоопа́рк) · 2 з (музе́й) · 3 с (сок) · 4 з (коза́) · 5 с (коса́) ·
6 с (спаси́бо)
