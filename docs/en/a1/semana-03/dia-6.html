Review: reading by the rules

Повторе́ние: чита́ем по пра́вилам

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today there's nothing new: you put together the week's four reading rules and the intonations, and test yourself. By the end you'll know:

  • which rule you already apply without thinking and which one still slips;
  • whether your questions with кто and with а sound different;
  • how much of the mini-test you get right without looking anything up.

1Warm-up0–3 min

One word from each day of the week. Read them aloud and, before each one, find the stressed syllable: it's the one in charge.

ру́чка — тетра́дь — ло́шадь — моро́з — сосе́дка

Now say them again, faster, clapping on the stressed syllable.

2Pronunciation3–8 min

The week's four rules, out loud. With them you can read almost any Russian word.

1. Unstressed о and а weaken. The о sounds [a], and at the end of a word the а is short and slack:

Москва́ — окно́ — ру́чка — ла́мпа — ша́пка

2. Unstressed е and я sound like a short [и]:

метро́ — телефо́н — тетра́дь — язы́к

3. The vowel or the ь after a consonant tells you whether it's hard or soft. And ж, ш, ц are always hard:

лук — люк · рад — ряд · брат — брать · маши́на

4. At the end of a word, the consonant loses its voice:

хлеб — сад — нож — снег — моро́з

And the intonations. A statement falls (ИК-1); a yes/no question jumps up and drops (ИК-3, from week 1); and the two new ones:

Э́то ру́чка. — Э́то ру́чка?
Кто э́то? — Что э́то?
А э́то? — А вы?

3Mini-test8–23 min

Don't look back at the earlier days. Choose one answer for each question: on the website, tap it; on paper, note the letter. The key is at the end.

1. Reading

How is it said?

  1. телефо́н → а) [te-le-FON] · б) [ti-LI-fon] · в) [ti-li-FON]
  2. хлеб → а) [khlyep] · б) [khlyeb] · в) [khlep]
  3. маши́на → а) [ma-SHY-na] · б) [ma-SHEE-na] · в) [MA-shi-na]
  4. ло́шадь → а) [LO-shad'] · б) [LO-shat'] · в) [LO-shat]
  5. моро́з → а) [mo-ROS] · б) [ma-ROS] · в) [ma-ROZ]

2. Words

What does it mean?

  1. тетра́дь → а) exercise book · б) pencil · в) backpack
  2. ключ → а) umbrella · б) hat · в) key
  3. лук → а) salt · б) door · в) onion
  4. снег → а) frost · б) snow · в) tooth
  5. подру́га → а) girlfriend (romantic) · б) neighbour (a woman) · в) friend (a woman)

3. Dialogues and intonation

Choose the reply that fits.

  1. — Э́то ру́чка? — … → а) Нет, э́то не ру́чка, а каранда́ш. · б) Нет, э́то нет ру́чка. · в) Не, э́то каранда́ш.
  2. Э́то не кни́га, … слова́рь. → а) и · б) нет · в) а
  3. — Кто э́то? — … → а) Э́то соль. · б) Э́то сосе́дка, Ни́на. · в) Да, э́то сосе́д.
  4. How does А вы? sound? → а) it falls, like a statement (ИК-1) · б) it rises and stays up (ИК-4) · в) it leans on а and falls (ИК-2)
  5. How does Что э́то? sound? → а) it leans on что and falls (ИК-2) · б) it jumps up on э́ and drops (ИК-3) · в) it rises and stays up (ИК-4)

Count your right answers. With 13 or more, the week is done. With 10 to 12, go back to the days the key points to for each mistake. With fewer than 10, reread days 2, 3 and 4 before moving on: they're the rules you'll read the whole course with.

4Your turn23–29 min

Read like a Russian. Copy these six words by hand and mark them in three colours (or with three signs, if you have no colours):

  • a circle round the stressed vowel;
  • a line under every unstressed о, а, е or я, the ones that weaken;
  • a cross on the final consonant that loses its voice.

тетра́дь · телефо́н · хлеб · моро́з · сосе́д · ло́шадь

Check against the key. Then read them aloud and record yourself: can you hear what you marked?

To finish, this mini-dialogue, with its intonation:

— Кто э́то?
— Э́то сосе́д. А э́то?
— Э́то не де́вушка, а ма́льчик.

5Wrap-up29–30 min

This week: four rules for reading any Russian word, two new intonations (ИК-2 and ИК-4) and 37 words.

Tomorrow, culture day: the words Russian brought in from other languages.

Show the answers

Mini-test: 1 в (day 2) · 2 а (day 4) · 3 а (day 3) · 4 б (day 3) · 5 б (day 4) · 6 а (day 2) · 7 в (day 1) · 8 в (day 3) · 9 б (day 4) · 10 в (day 5) · 11 а (day 1) · 12 в (day 1) · 13 б (day 5) · 14 б (day 5) · 15 а (day 5)

The traps. In 2, [khlyeb] keeps the voice at the end, the English habit, and [khlep] forgets that е softens the л. In 3, ш is always hard, so the и after it sounds like ы. In 4, you can hear the ь: the final т is soft, and voiceless too. In 5 there are two rules at once: the unstressed о sounds [a] and the final з sounds [s]. In 10, подру́га is a friend; a romantic girlfriend is моя́ де́вушка. In 11, нет answers and не negates the word after it.

Your turn.

  • тетра́дь [ti-TRAT']: stress on the а; the е weakens; the final дь sounds [t'].
  • телефо́н [ti-li-FON]: stress on the о; both е's weaken.
  • хлеб [khlyep]: stress on the е, the only vowel; the final б sounds [p].
  • моро́з [ma-ROS]: stress on the second о; the first one weakens; the final з sounds [s].
  • сосе́д [sa-SYET]: stress on the е; the о weakens; the final д sounds [t].
  • ло́шадь [LO-shat']: stress on the о; the а weakens; the final дь sounds [t'].
