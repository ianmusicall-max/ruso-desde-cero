> **Today** you ask for and give a phone number, digit by digit. By the end you'll have read a full unit-7 scene without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**A phone number isn't said in one breath, it's spelled out in groups.**
In Russian, just like in English, it's usually said two or three digits
at a time, and the listener repeats it back to confirm before writing it
down.

Before you read, think: when someone reads you a number over the phone,
what do you say to make sure you got it right?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

*Nina asks Tom in the courtyard. Both numbers in this dialogue are made
up, for practice.*

> **Ни́на:** Том, у тебя́ есть телефо́н?
> **Том:** Да, есть. А у тебя́?
> **Ни́на:** Да. Како́й у тебя́ но́мер?
> **Том:** Шесть, оди́н, во́семь, три.
> **Ни́на:** Повтори́те, пожа́луйста.
> **Том:** Шесть, оди́н, во́семь, три.
> **Ни́на:** Шесть, оди́н, во́семь, три. Пра́вильно?
> **Том:** Да, пра́вильно! А твой но́мер?
> **Ни́на:** Два, семь, четы́ре, шесть.
> **Том:** Два, семь, четы́ре, шесть?
> **Ни́на:** Да, пра́вильно.
> **Том:** Спаси́бо, Ни́на!
> **Ни́на:** Пожа́луйста!

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**No new words today**: the whole episode comes from units 1 to 7. This
is the only question worth explaining:

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Како́й у тебя́ но́мер? | What's your number? | Како́й у тебя́ но́мер? |

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Tom's number is 6183.
2. Nina's number is 2746.
3. Tom confirms Nina's number by repeating it in full.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Како́й у тебя́ но́мер?
2. Повтори́те, пожа́луйста.

### В. Your own number

*Say your own phone number out loud, digit by digit (or make one up, if
you'd rather), using the numbers you already know.*

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** how to ask for and confirm a phone number digit by digit, and that **повтори́те** works for a number just as well as for anything else you didn't catch.

This closes the first week of unit 7. Next week, **чей** and giving an
address.
