> **Today** you keep practicing the change, with the verb **слу́шать** and one new word. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - ask **Что ты слу́шаешь?**;
> - use **ра́дио**, which never changes;
> - review джаз and рок as objects.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The whole verb, from memory:

> слу́шаю — слу́шаешь — слу́шает — слу́шаем — слу́шаете — слу́шают

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Ра́дио** ends in an unstressed **-о**, like **кака́о**: it barely
weakens, because it's a foreign word Russian borrowed as is.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> ра́дио — му́зыку — пе́сню

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Ра́дио** is new today, and it has one advantage: **it never
changes**, in any role. It's one of those foreign words Russian doesn't
decline.

| Word | It sounds like | It means |
|---|---|---|
| ра́дио | **RA**-dio | radio |

> — Что ты слу́шаешь?
> *What are you listening to?*
> — Я слу́шаю ра́дио. А ты?
> *I'm listening to the radio. And you?*
> — Я слу́шаю му́зыку. Люблю́ джаз.
> *I'm listening to music. I love jazz.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English doesn't decline "radio" either, so the two languages agree
here: it's the one exception that costs you nothing.**

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| I'm listening to the radio. | Я слу́шаю ра́дио. |
| It's the radio. | Э́то ра́дио. |

There's no trap in this word: enjoy it while it lasts, because the
change comes back tomorrow with a new verb.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я слу́шаю ра́дио. · Я слу́шаю му́зыку. · Я слу́шаю пе́сню.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. I'm listening to the radio.
2. What are you listening to?
3. I'm listening to music.

### В. Does it change or not?

*Does this word change as a direct object?*

1. ра́дио → а) yes · б) no
2. му́зыка → а) yes · б) no
3. футбо́л → а) yes · б) no

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your imaginary radio.** Say out loud what you're listening to:
ра́дио, му́зыку, джаз or рок, in four different sentences.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Что ты слу́шаешь? · Я слу́шаю ра́дио. · Я слу́шаю му́зыку.
>
> **Now I know:** **ра́дио** never changes, just like in English.

Tomorrow: a new verb, **смотре́ть**.
