> **Today there are no new words: there's an order.** You put together four words you already know separately —а́дрес, у́лица, дом, кварти́ра— in the order a Russian address is actually given in. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - give a full address, in the right order;
> - use the compound numbers from unit 6 inside an address;
> - follow a Russian address read out in full.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The four words, each on its own, out loud:

> а́дрес — у́лица — дом — кварти́ра

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Review the compound numbers: **два́дцать четы́ре**, **три́дцать оди́н**.
Today they go inside an address, not on their own.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> у́лица, дом два́дцать четы́ре, кварти́ра три́дцать оди́н

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**No new vocabulary: the order is what changes.** A Russian address is
given like this, in one line, with no prepositions between the parts:

> у́лица [name], дом [number], кварти́ра [number]

> — Како́й у вас а́дрес?
> *What's your address?*
> — У́лица Пу́шкина, дом два́дцать четы́ре, кварти́ра три́дцать оди́н.
> *Pushkin Street, building 24, apartment 31.*
> — Повтори́те, пожа́луйста.
> *Repeat that, please.*
> — У́лица Пу́шкина, дом два́дцать четы́ре, кварти́ра три́дцать оди́н.
> — Пра́вильно?
> *Right?*
> — Да, пра́вильно.

The street in the example is made up, for practice.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English usually links the parts with "number" and "apartment";
Russian just lines the words up, with no connectors.**

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| Pushkin Street, number 24, apartment 31 | у́лица Пу́шкина, дом два́дцать четы́ре, кварти́ра три́дцать оди́н |

There's no "the number of" or "the apartment of": each word already
says what it is, and you just say them in order.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, with the example numbers.*

> У́лица Пу́шкина, дом два́дцать четы́ре, кварти́ра три́дцать оди́н.

### Б. Put the parts in order.

*Number 1 to 3, in the order they're said.*

1. кварти́ра
2. у́лица
3. дом

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Како́й у вас а́дрес?
2. У́лица Пу́шкина, дом пять.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your own address.** Read out an address out loud —yours, or one you
make up— with **у́лица**, **дом** and **кварти́ра**, in that order.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** how to give a full address in the right order, using the compound numbers from unit 6.

Tomorrow: the two sounds **ч** and **щ**.
