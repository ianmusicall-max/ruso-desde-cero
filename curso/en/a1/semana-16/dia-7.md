> **Today** it's Friday night, and Camila, Tom and Misha can't agree on what to watch. By the end you'll have read a full unit-8 scene without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**A Friday night with clashing tastes doesn't always end in a fight:
sometimes it ends with everyone doing their own thing, at the same
time.** Today you'll see how three people with three different hobbies
solve the same evening without anyone missing out on what they want.

Before you read, think: if you had to choose between watching a movie
and listening to a match, can you think of a way to do both?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Эпизо́д 8. Футбо́л и́ли кино́?

*Friday night. Camila, Tom and Misha are at Kira's place.*

> **Ками́ла:** Что ты де́лаешь, Том?
> **Том:** Я смотрю́ фильм. Ты лю́бишь кино́?
> **Ками́ла:** Да, люблю́. Но я о́чень люблю́ футбо́л!
> **Том:** Ой, я ненави́жу футбо́л.
> **Ми́ша:** А я люблю́ и фильм, и футбо́л.
> **Ками́ла:** Хорошо́. Ты смо́тришь фильм. Я слу́шаю ра́дио: там футбо́л.
> **Том:** А ты, Ми́ша?
> **Ми́ша:** Я смотрю́ фильм! И я слу́шаю ра́дио!

*Camila and Tom laugh.*

> **Том:** Ты лю́бишь и фильм, и футбо́л, Ми́ша?
> **Ми́ша:** Да! Люблю́!

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**No new words today**: the whole episode comes from units 1 to 8.

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. Tom likes football.
2. Camila listens to football on the radio.
3. Misha only watches the movie.

### Б. Найди́те реше́ние.

*Camila and Tom can't watch two things on the same screen at once. How does Camila solve the problem, without giving up either thing she likes?*

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Я слу́шаю ра́дио: там футбо́л.
2. Ты лю́бишь и фильм, и футбо́л?

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** that **и… и…** means "both X and Y," and that you don't always have to choose between two hobbies if you find another way to have both.
>
> This closes unit 8. Next week, unit 9.

## Answers

**А.** 1 false: he hates it · 2 true · 3 false: she watches the movie
and listens to the radio at the same time

**Б.** Tom watches the movie and Camila listens to football on the
radio, at the same time. Neither of them has to give up what they
like.

**В.** 1 I'm listening to the radio: football's on there. · 2 Do you
like both the movie and football?
