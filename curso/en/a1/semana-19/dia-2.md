> **Today** you learn the case that says WHERE something is: the prepositional. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **в** + the place, with the **-е** ending;
> - say where each character is;
> - ask **Где?** and answer with the new chunk.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's six words, from memory:

> дом · кварти́ра · ко́мната · ку́хня · спа́льня · гости́ная

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Ко́мнате** and **спа́льне** change their last letter when saying
WHERE; the stress doesn't move from its place.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> в ко́мнате — в спа́льне — в до́ме — в кварти́ре

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**To say WHERE something is INSIDE a place, use в + the noun with a
new ending.** It's a new case, the prepositional, and today you see
its most common form:

| Dictionary form | Where (prepositional) |
|---|---|
| ко́мната | в ко́мнат**е** |
| спа́льня | в спа́льн**е** |
| кварти́ра | в кварти́р**е** |
| дом | в до́м**е** |

**Today's rule**: if the noun ends in **-а** or **-я**, that letter
is replaced by **-е**. If it ends in a consonant (like **дом**),
**-е** is simply added.

> — Где Том?
> *Where is Tom?*
> — Том в ко́мнате. А Ками́ла в спа́льне.
> *Tom is in the room. And Camila is in the bedroom.*
> — А вы где?
> *And where are you (all)?*
> — Мы в кварти́ре. Ки́ра в до́ме.
> *We're in the apartment. Kira is at home.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English uses "in" without changing the noun; Russian uses **в**
AND changes the noun's ending.** Both things happen at once, and
both are needed:

| English (only the preposition matters) | Russian (both things change) |
|---|---|
| IN the room | **в** ко́мнат**е** |
| IN the apartment | **в** кварти́р**е** |

If you say just "в ко́мната," without changing the ending, you'll
be understood, but it sounds like a beginner's mistake: the ending
matters as much as the preposition.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Том в ко́мнате. Ками́ла в спа́льне. Мы в кварти́ре.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian, changing the ending.*

1. Tom is in the kitchen. (ку́хня)
2. I'm at home. (дом)
3. We're in the apartment. (кварти́ра)

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Где Ки́ра? Ки́ра в до́ме.
2. Мы в ко́мнате, а Том в спа́льне.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Where you are.** Say out loud where you are right now, with **в**
and the correct ending for your room.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Том в ко́мнате. · Ками́ла в спа́льне. · Мы в кварти́ре.
>
> **Now I know:** the prepositional case with **в**, and that **-а/-я** becomes **-е**.
