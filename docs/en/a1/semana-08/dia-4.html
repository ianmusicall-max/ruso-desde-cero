A1 · Unit 4 · Week 8 · Day 4 · Lesson

The consonant that changes inside a word

Звук, кото́рый меня́ется

Draft: this lesson hasn't had its final Russian review yet. It may change.

Today you find out why авто́бус sounds [af-TO-boos] and вокза́л, [vag-ZAL]: inside a word, consonants catch each other's voicing. By the end you'll be able to:

  • read any pair of consonants correctly;
  • know which of the two is in charge;
  • fix three words you already know and have been saying wrong.

1Warm-up0–3 min

Yesterday's conversation, all of it, from memory:

— Извини́те, пожа́луйста! Где здесь банк? — Вот он, спра́ва. — Поня́тно, спаси́бо!

2Pronunciation3–8 min

In unit 2 you learnt that at the end of a word a voiced consonant goes quiet: хлеб [khlyep]. Today, what happens inside.

The second one is in charge. When two consonants sit together, the first one adjusts to the second:

If the second is… The first… Example
voiceless (п, т, к, с, ш, ф, ч, ц) goes quiet авто́бус [af-TO-boos]
voiced (б, д, г, з, ж) turns on its voice вокза́л [vag-ZAL]

Слу́шайте и повторя́йте. Listen and repeat, watching the letter on the page and the sound you make:

авто́бус [af-TO-boos] · ло́жка [LOSH-ka] · ска́зка [SKAS-ka]
вокза́л [vag-ZAL] · футбо́л [food-BOL] · сде́лать [ZDYE-lat']
ло́дка [LOT-ka] · ю́бка [YUP-ka] · остано́вка [as-ta-NOF-ka]

3New8–18 min

Start with three you already know. You've had them since units 1 and 2, and you have almost certainly been saying them letter by letter:

Word ✗ As it's written ✓ As it's said
авто́бус av-to-bus af-TO-boos
футбо́л foot-bol food-BOL
вокза́л vak-sal vag-ZAL

And now six new ones, each with its own trap:

Word Say it Means
ло́жка LOSH-ka spoon
ска́зка SKAS-ka fairy tale
ло́дка LOT-ka boat
сде́лать ZDYE-lat' to do, to make
ю́бка YUP-ka skirt
остано́вка as-ta-NOF-ka stop (bus, tram)

All of them are written with one letter and said with another, and the spelling isn't lying: ло́жка is written with ж because in other forms of the word the ж is heard again. Russian writes the whole word and lets the catching happen in the mouth.

— Где авто́бус?
Where's the bus?
— Авто́бус? Вот он, спра́ва. А вокза́л далеко́.
The bus? There it is, on the right. The station is far away.

One comfortable exception: nothing happens before в. Два still sounds [dva] and свой, [svoy]: в doesn't pass anything on.

⚠️ Contrast with English

English does exactly the same thing and nobody told you. Say "cats" and then "dogs": the same letter -s is [s] after a voiceless t and [z] after a voiced g. Say "have to" at normal speed and it comes out [HAF-ta]. So the skill is already yours; what you have to learn is where it happens in Russian.

Word ✗ Letter by letter ✓ Said
авто́бус [av-TO-boos] [af-TO-boos]
вокза́л [vak-SAL] [vag-ZAL]
футбо́л [foot-BOL] [food-BOL]

And watch "football" against футбо́л, which also moves its stress: English leans on the first syllable, Russian on the second.

4Practice18–25 min

А. Чита́йте.

Read aloud, letting the consonants catch.

авто́бус · ло́жка · ска́зка · вокза́л · футбо́л · ло́дка
сде́лать · ю́бка · остано́вка

Б. Глухо́й и́ли зво́нкий?

Does the consonant in bold go quiet or turn its voice on?

  1. авто́бус
  2. вокза́л
  3. ло́жка
  4. ю́бка
  5. ска́зка
  6. сде́лать

В. Как говори́м?

Choose the right pronunciation.

  1. ло́дка → а) [LOD-ka] · б) [LOT-ka]
  2. вокза́л → а) [vak-ZAL] · б) [vag-ZAL]
  3. ло́жка → а) [LOSH-ka] · б) [LOZH-ka]
  4. авто́бус → а) [af-TO-boos] · б) [av-TO-boos]
  5. остано́вка → а) [as-ta-NOV-ka] · б) [as-ta-NOF-ka]

5Your turn25–29 min

Hunt for traps. Take ten Russian words you already know and look for two consonants in a row. Circle the second one —the one in charge— and say the word aloud:

ло́жка · где · вокза́л · ска́зка · футбо́л · остано́вка

Then read them while recording yourself. Listen: does вокза́л come out with a [g], or does it stay [k] the way it's written?

6Wrap-up29–30 min

I can now say:
ло́жка, ска́зка, ло́дка, сде́лать, ю́бка, остано́вка
—and at last авто́бус, футбо́л and вокза́л properly—

I know: inside a word the second consonant is in charge: before a voiceless one the first goes quiet, before a voiced one it turns its voice on. And nothing happens before в.
