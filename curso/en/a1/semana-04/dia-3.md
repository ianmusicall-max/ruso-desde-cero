> **Today** you read words you understand without having studied them: **компью́тер**, **авто́бус**, **аэропо́рт**… The trap isn't the meaning: it's the stress. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read international words fluently, with all the unit's rules;
> - put the stress where Russian puts it, which is often not where English does;
> - know why **кафе́** sounds [ka-FE] and not [ka-FYE].

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's café, aloud, with two different drinks:

> — Что вам? — Мне кака́о, пожа́луйста. — Вот, пожа́луйста. А вам? — Мне квас.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**The е that doesn't soften.** In almost every Russian word, е softens the
consonant in front of it: **нет** [nyet], **телефо́н** [ti-li-FON]. But in
some foreign words the consonant stays hard, and the е sounds like the e in
"bed":

| Word | Said |
|---|---|
| кафе́ | [ka-FE] |
| интерне́т | [in-ter-NET] |
| компью́тер | [kam-PYOO-ter] |

No rule tells you which words do it: you learn it word by word. In this
course, whenever it happens, we'll tell you.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat: the hard ones first, then the soft ones.*

> кафе́ — интерне́т — компью́тер
> нет — телефо́н — метро́

And one more, as a bonus: in **авто́бус** the в sounds [f], [af-TO-boos],
because it comes before a т. Why that happens, you'll see in unit 4.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

Eight words you already understand:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| компью́тер | kam-**PYOO**-ter | computer |
| интерне́т | in-ter-**NET** | internet |
| телеви́зор | ti-li-**VEE**-zar | television set, TV |
| авто́бус | af-**TO**-boos | bus |
| трамва́й | tram-**VAI** | tram, streetcar |
| сала́т | sa-**LAT** | salad |
| аэропо́рт | a-e-ra-**PORT** | airport |
| пробле́ма | pra-**BLYE**-ma | problem |

> — Что э́то? Телефо́н?
> *What's this? A phone?*
> — Нет, э́то не телефо́н, а компью́тер.
> *No, it's not a phone, it's a computer.*
> — А э́то?
> *And this?*
> — Э́то телеви́зор.
> *A TV.*

Now compare where the beat falls in English and in Russian:

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| **SAL**ad | сал**а́**т |
| **AIR**port | аэроп**о́**рт |
| **IN**ternet | интерн**е́**т |
| **PROB**lem | пробл**е́**ма |
| **TEL**evision | телев**и́**зор |
| com**PU**ter | компь**ю́**тер |

In **компью́тер** it matches; in the others, it doesn't. The word looks
familiar, and that's exactly why your mouth puts the English stress on it
without asking.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English gives you the word and takes away the stress.** English likes the
beat at the front: SALad, AIRport, PROBlem. Russian puts it where it puts
it, and a misplaced stress changes the whole word, because different vowels
weaken:

| Word | ✗ Your mouth says | ✓ It's said |
|---|---|---|
| сала́т | ~~[SA-lat]~~ | [sa-LAT] |
| пробле́ма | ~~[PRO-bli-ma]~~ | [pra-BLYE-ma] |
| интерне́т | ~~[IN-ter-net]~~ | [in-ter-NET] |

**And no "ye" in кафе́.** The е of these words is a plain e: **кафе́** is
[ka-FE], with nothing slipping in before the e.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read fluently, clapping on the stressed syllable.*

> компью́тер — интерне́т — телеви́зор — авто́бус — трамва́й — сала́т — аэропо́рт — пробле́ма

### Б. Где ударе́ние?

*Where's the stress? Only one of the three forms is right.*

1. а) те́левизор · б) телеви́зор · в) телевизо́р
2. а) авто́бус · б) а́втобус · в) автобу́с
3. а) ко́мпьютер · б) компьюте́р · в) компью́тер
4. а) а́эропорт · б) аэропо́рт · в) аэро́порт

### В. Прочита́йте и отве́тьте.

*Read, and say whether each sentence is true or false.*

> Э́то аэропо́рт. Вот авто́бус, а там такси́. А э́то кафе́. Там ко́фе, чай и сала́т. И там интерне́т!

1. It's a railway station.
2. There's a bus and a taxi.
3. There's salad in the café.
4. There's no internet in the café.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your town in international words.** Write five short sentences about your
neighbourhood or your town, with **Вот**, **Там** and today's words:

> Вот авто́бус. Там трамва́й. Э́то не кафе́, а рестора́н…

Read them aloud and record yourself. Listen for one thing only: does the beat
fall where Russian puts it, or where English would?

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> компью́тер, интерне́т, телеви́зор, авто́бус, трамва́й, сала́т, аэропо́рт, пробле́ма
>
> **I know:** the word looks familiar, the stress often doesn't; and in some foreign words е doesn't soften: **кафе́** [ka-FE].
