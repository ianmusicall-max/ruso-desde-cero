> **Today there are no new words: there's a floor plan.** You read the description of a whole apartment, with every object in its place, and check how much you can understand without a dictionary.

## 1. Before you read (0–5 min)

**A floor plan describes a whole space in a few short sentences,
one after another.** You don't need to understand every word to
find your way: it's enough to follow, room by room, what's there
and where.

Before reading, think: if you described your own home in six
sentences, which room would you start with?

## 2. Text (5–18 min)

### Пла́н кварти́ры

> Вот кварти́ра. Э́то ку́хня. Стол на ку́хне. Ла́мпа на столе́. Телефо́н
> то́же на столе́. Э́то спа́льня. Су́мка в шкафу́. Ключи́ на полу́. А э́то
> балко́н. Том на балко́не.

## 3. Words (18–23 min)

**There isn't a single new word today**: the whole floor plan
comes from this week and from earlier units.

| Russian | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Вот кварти́ра. | Here's the apartment. | Вот кварти́ра. |

## 4. Task (23–29 min)

### А. Ве́рно и́ли неве́рно?

*True or false?*

1. The lamp is on the table.
2. The keys are in the cupboard.
3. Tom is on the balcony.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Телефо́н то́же на столе́.
2. Су́мка в шкафу́.

### В. Your own floor plan

*Write (or say out loud) three sentences about your own home, following the model: a room, an object, and where it is.*

## 5. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now you know:** that **то́же** ("also") joins a second sentence to the first without repeating the verb, and that a whole floor plan can be described with the words you already have.

Tomorrow: the living room and the bathroom.

## Answers

**А.** 1 true · 2 false: they're on the floor · 3 true

**Б.** 1 The phone is also on the table. · 2 The bag is in the cupboard.
