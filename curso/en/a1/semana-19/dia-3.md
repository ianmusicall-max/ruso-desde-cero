> **Today** you learn the other location preposition, for surfaces. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **на** + the object, with the **-е** ending;
> - name **по́лка**, the only new word;
> - compare **на** (surface) with yesterday's **в** (inside).

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's chunk, from memory:

> в ко́мнате · в до́ме · в кварти́ре

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Столе́** moves the stress to the end, something **стол** alone
didn't do; **по́лке** doesn't move anything.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> на столе́ — на по́лке — на дива́не

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**To say something is ON a surface, use на + the same prepositional
case from yesterday.** The ending is formed the same way
—consonant + **-е**, or **-а** becomes **-е**— except **стол** also
moves the stress to the end:

| Dictionary form | Where (prepositional) |
|---|---|
| стол | на стол**е́** |
| по́лка | на по́лк**е** |
| дива́н | на дива́н**е** |

> — Где ключи́?
> *Where are the keys?*
> — Ключи́ на столе́. А письмо́ на по́лке.
> *The keys are on the table. And the letter is on the shelf.*
> — А су́мка?
> *And the bag?*
> — Су́мка на дива́не.
> *The bag is on the sofa.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English says "on" for a surface and "in" for inside; Russian
never mixes the two prepositions.**

| Inside (yesterday) | On (today) |
|---|---|
| **в** ко́мнат**е** | **на** стол**е́** |
| **в** до́м**е** | **на** дива́н**е** |

Decide first whether something is INSIDE or ON, then choose **в**
or **на**; the **-е** ending is the same either way.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Ключи́ на столе́. Телефо́н на столе́. Ру́чка на по́лке.

### Б. В и́ли на?

*Choose the correct preposition.*

1. Том ______ ко́мнате.
2. Письмо́ ______ по́лке.
3. Ки́ра ______ до́ме.
4. Су́мка ______ дива́не.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Где телефо́н? Телефо́н на столе́.
2. Ру́чка на по́лке, а су́мка на дива́не.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your table.** Say out loud which objects are **на столе́** right
now, next to you.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Ключи́ на столе́. · Письмо́ на по́лке. · Су́мка на дива́не.
>
> **Now I know:** to use **на** for surfaces, with the same **-е** ending as **в**.

Tomorrow: the kitchen and the balcony.

## Answers

**Б.** 1 в · 2 на · 3 в · 4 на

**В.** 1 Where is the phone? The phone is on the table. · 2 The pen
is on the shelf, and the bag is on the sofa.
