*Choose the correct sentence.*

**The traps.** In 6, 7 and 10, the OBJECT's gender decides the ending
—ла́мпа is feminine, стол is masculine, ключи́ is plural— not yours. In
8, **его́** never changes for the object: any added ending ("его́а,"
"его́е," "его́и") is wrong. In 12, English reads @ as "at"; Russian
reads it as "соба́ка," after the shape of its tail. In 15, **щ** can be
held for a moment, unlike **ч**, which starts sharply and doesn't
stretch.
