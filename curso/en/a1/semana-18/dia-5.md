> **Today** you complete the quartet of quantity and quality adverbs, with two new words and two review ones. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **мно́го** and **ма́ло** to say how much;
> - use **хорошо́** and **пло́хо** again to say how;
> - combine all four with any verb from this week.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's two forms, from memory:

> нра́вится · нра́вятся

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Мно́го** and **ма́ло** are stressed on the first syllable, unlike
many of this week's words.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> мно́го — ма́ло — хорошо́ — пло́хо

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Хорошо́ and пло́хо you already know: they say HOW you do
something. Мно́го and ма́ло are new, and they say HOW MUCH you do
it.** All four work the same way, next to the verb:

| Word | Says | Example |
|---|---|---|
| мно́го | a lot | Я мно́го путеше́ствую. |
| ма́ло | little | Он ма́ло поёт. |
| хорошо́ | well | Ты хорошо́ игра́ешь на гита́ре. |
| пло́хо | badly | Я пло́хо танцу́ю. |

> — Ты мно́го рису́ешь?
> *Do you draw a lot?*
> — Да, мно́го. Но игра́ю на гита́ре ма́ло.
> *Yes, a lot. But I play the guitar very little.*
> — А гото́вишь хорошо́ и́ли пло́хо?
> *And do you cook well or badly?*
> — Гото́влю хорошо́!
> *I cook well!*

**Немно́го**, which you already know, is different from **мно́го**:
немно́го means "a little" (a small amount that's still there), while
ма́ло means "too little" (an amount that feels insufficient). They
aren't opposites: немно́го sits between ма́ло and мно́го.

**Ча́сто** ("often") completes the group: it says how frequently,
not how much or how well.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English orders these four adverbs next to the verb just like
Russian does, so there's no word-order trap here.** The one thing
worth noting is that Russian doesn't change мно́го for gender or
number the way English sometimes changes "much" to "many."

| English (changes with the noun) | Russian (invariable) |
|---|---|
| much, many, a lot of | always мно́го |

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я мно́го рабо́таю. Ты ма́ло отдыха́ешь. Он хорошо́ пи́шет.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. I work a lot.
2. You rest little.
3. He cooks well.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ты мно́го путеше́ствуешь?
2. Он пло́хо спит.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Four sentences.** Combine мно́го, ма́ло, хорошо́, and пло́хо, one
each, with four different verbs from this unit.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Я мно́го путеше́ствую. · Он ма́ло поёт. · Гото́влю хорошо́.
>
> **Now I know:** мно́го/ма́ло say how much, хорошо́/пло́хо say how, and all four are invariable.
