> **Today** you learn three more tricky letters and the golden rule of Russian pronunciation. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - read **В, Е, Н**;
> - read **метро́**, **рестора́н**, **раке́та** and **Москва́**, and know why Москва́ sounds [mas-KVA];
> - say **вот** ("here's") and **нет** ("no").

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Read yesterday's words aloud, quickly:

> сок · торт · ка́рта · ма́ска · ко́смос · у́тро · у́хо · рука́

Did a **С** or a **Р** catch you out? Notice how the last о of **у́тро** and
**ко́смос** sounded: today you find out why.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Three more letters that **look** English and aren't:

| Letter | Looks like | Sounds | As in |
|---|---|---|---|
| В в | B | [v] | **v**an |
| Е е | E | [ye] | **ye**s; after a consonant it softens it: **не** ≈ "nye" |
| Н н | H | [n] | **n**o |

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> ва — во — ву · на — но — ну
> ве — не — ме — те — ре

**Е after a consonant.** In **не** the [n] and the [ye] melt into one sound,
the way *n* and *y* do in *canyon*. Don't say "nuh-yeh" in two beats: it's
one quick syllable.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

With 12 letters you can read words you understand at a glance. Start with a
classic: on a Russian street you'll see **РЕСТОРА́Н**, and your eye will read
"PECTOPAH". Now you can read it:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| нет | nyet | no |
| вот | vot | here's, there's (pointing) |
| метро́ | mi-**TRO** | metro, underground |
| рестора́н | ri-sta-**RAN** | restaurant |
| раке́та | ra-**KYE**-ta | rocket |
| Москва́ | mas-**KVA** | Moscow |
| окно́ | ak-**NO** | window |
| ко́мната | **KOM**-na-ta | room |

One-syllable words (**вот**, **нет**, **кот**) carry no mark: the stress has
nowhere else to go.

**The golden rule: an unstressed о sounds [a].** Look at **окно́**: it has
two о's, and only the stressed one sounds [o]; the other sounds [a]. The
same in **Москва́** [mas-**KVA**] and **рестора́н** [ri-sta-**RAN**]. An
unstressed е weakens too, towards a short [i]: **метро́** [mi-**TRO**]. Unit
2 deals with that; for now, just don't say it loud.

And two words for every day. **Вот** points at something you see or hand
over; **нет** is "no":

> — **Вот** метро́!
> *Here's the metro!*
>
> — Там рестора́н?
> *Is there a restaurant there?*
> — **Нет**, там метро́.
> *No, the metro's there.*

The question uses the same words as the answer: only your voice turns it
into a question. You'll practise that in lesson 5.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**Familiar words, Russian stress.** Russian took these words from other
languages and put the stress where it pleased:

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| **me**tro | метро́ [mi-**TRO**] |
| **res**taurant | рестора́н [ri-sta-**RAN**] |
| **rock**et | раке́та [ra-**KYE**-ta] |

Say them with the English stress and a Russian has to think twice. Learn
each word with its stress, as if the stress were one more letter.

**The good news: you already reduce vowels.** In *banana* only the middle
*a* is a full vowel; the other two are weak. Russian does the same with о:
the stressed one is a full [o], the others weaken to [a] — **Москва́**
[mas-**KVA**], **окно́** [ak-**NO**]. The catch is knowing which syllable is
stressed, and that's why this course marks it on every word.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud. Before each word, find the stressed о: all the others sound [a].*

> окно́ — ко́мната — Москва́ — метро́ — рестора́н — ко́смос — вот — нет — Анто́н — Ве́ра

### Б. [о] и́ли [а]?

*Say how the bold о sounds: [о] or [а].*

1. М**о**сква́
2. **о**кно́
3. к**о́**мната
4. ко́см**о**с
5. Ант**о́**н
6. окн**о́**

### В. Соедини́те.

*Match each word with its translation.*

1. окно́
2. ко́мната
3. раке́та
4. нет
5. вот

а) room · б) no · в) here's · г) rocket · д) window

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Tour guide.** Imagine you're showing a Russian friend round your town, map
in hand. Point and present, with **вот** and **там**:

> Вот ка́рта. Вот метро́. А там — рестора́н.
> *Here's the map. Here's the metro. And over there, a restaurant.*
>
> — Там Москва́?
> — Нет!

Then write **В, Е, Н** by hand, capital and small, and today's eight words.
Circle every unstressed о: those sound [a].

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> Вот метро́! · Нет, там рестора́н. · Москва́ [mas-KVA].
>
> **I know:** А, О, К, М, Т, С, Р, У, Х, В, Е, Н — 12 of 33 letters.

Tomorrow: the letter И and your first real conversation: what's your name?

## Answers

**А.** Only the stressed о's sound [о]: окн**о́**, к**о́**мната, метр**о́**,
к**о́**смос, Ант**о́**н, and the one in **вот**, the only vowel in its word.
All the others sound [а]: **о**кно́, М**о**сква́, рест**о**ра́н, ко́см**о**с.

**Б.** 1 [а] · 2 [а] · 3 [о] · 4 [а] · 5 [о] · 6 [о]

**В.** 1 д (окно́ — window) · 2 а (ко́мната — room) · 3 г (раке́та — rocket) ·
4 б (нет — no) · 5 в (вот — here's)
