> **Today there are no new words: there's practice.** You bring together the verb **чита́ть** (which you've fully conjugated since unit 5) with yesterday's change. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - ask **Что ты чита́ешь?** and answer with the right object;
> - tell which objects change and which don't;
> - read a whole conversation without a single slip of form.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The whole verb, from memory:

> чита́ю — чита́ешь — чита́ет — чита́ем — чита́ете — чита́ют

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Газе́ту** sounds just like **газе́та**, only with a different last
vowel: spot it as you read aloud.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> газе́та — газе́ту
> журна́л — письмо́

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**No new grammar: the text below combines the verb чита́ть** (unit 5)
**with yesterday's change.** Журна́л and письмо́ are masculine and
neuter: they don't change. Газе́та is feminine: it becomes газе́ту.

> — Что ты чита́ешь?
> *What are you reading?*
> — Я чита́ю журна́л. А ты?
> *I'm reading a magazine. And you?*
> — Я чита́ю газе́ту.
> *I'm reading a newspaper.*
> — А Ки́ра чита́ет письмо́.
> *And Kira is reading a letter.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English has no such rule, so the risk is forgetting it right when the
noun is feminine.** Nothing unusual happens with a masculine or neuter
noun, which is exactly why it's easy to forget that a feminine one does
something different:

| Object | Does it change? |
|---|---|
| журна́л, письмо́ | no |
| газе́та → газе́ту | yes |

The trap isn't the ones that change: it's remembering that one in
three, in this list, actually does.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read the dialogue from "New" aloud, all three voices.*

### Б. Fill in журна́л, газе́ту or письмо́.

*Choose the correct object.*

1. Я чита́ю ______. (a magazine)
2. Ты чита́ешь ______? (a newspaper)
3. Она́ чита́ет ______. (a letter)

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Что ты чита́ешь?
2. Я чита́ю газе́ту.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Three objects, three sentences.** Say out loud what you're reading,
using журна́л, газе́ту and письмо́, one per sentence.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** журна́л and письмо́ don't change; газе́та becomes газе́ту, because it's feminine.
