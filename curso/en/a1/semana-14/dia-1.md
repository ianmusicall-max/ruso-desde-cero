> **Today** you learn to ask who owns something. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **чей, чья, чьё, чьи** depending on the noun you're asking about;
> - answer with the right possessive, with or without the noun;
> - understand the question before it's even finished.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's three unchanging forms, out loud:

> его́ — её — их

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Чей** and **чьи** sound a lot alike, but they don't end in the same
vowel.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> чей — чья — чьё — чьи
> Чей э́то стол?

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**The same logic as мой/твой/наш/ваш, but for asking.** The noun decides
the form of **чей**, just like it decided the form of **мой**.

| Word | Used with |
|---|---|
| чей | masculine |
| чья | feminine |
| чьё | neuter |
| чьи | plural |

> — Чей э́то стол?
> *Whose table is this?*
> — Мой.
> *Mine.*
> — Чья э́то су́мка?
> *Whose bag is this?*
> — Э́то её су́мка.
> *It's hers.*
> — Чьи э́то ключи́?
> *Whose keys are these?*
> — Его́.
> *His.*

**You can answer with just the possessive, skipping the noun**, if it's
already clear what's being talked about: **Мой.**, instead of **Мой
стол.**

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English has one form —"whose"— and Russian has four.** English leaves
the work to the answer; Russian already does it in the question itself,
because it anticipates the noun's gender:

| English (one form) | Russian (four forms) |
|---|---|
| Whose table is it? | Чей э́то стол? *(masculine)* |
| Whose bag is it? | Чья э́то су́мка? *(feminine)* |

Pick the wrong form of **чей** and the question sounds unfinished before
you're done saying it, the same way it would with **мой/моя́**.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Чей э́то стол? · Чья э́то су́мка? · Чьё э́то письмо́? · Чьи э́то ключи́?

### Б. Чей, чья, чьё и́ли чьи?

*Choose the correct form.*

1. ______ э́то брат?
2. ______ э́то сестра́?
3. ______ э́то письмо́?
4. ______ э́то ключи́?

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Чья э́то су́мка?
2. Чьи э́то часы́?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Four objects, four questions.** Pick four things from the last few
weeks (стол, су́мка, письмо́, ключи́) and ask "whose is it" with the
right form of **чей**, out loud.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Чей э́то стол? · Чья э́то су́мка? · Чьё э́то письмо́? · Чьи э́то ключи́?
>
> **Now I know:** the four forms of **чей**, and that you can answer with just the possessive.
