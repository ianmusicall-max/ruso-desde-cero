> **Today** you complete the verb **люби́ть** with the forms that were missing. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - ask **Ты лю́бишь…?** and, formally, **Вы лю́бите…?**;
> - report the result with **он лю́бит** and **она́ лю́бит**;
> - run a full survey about the unit's hobbies.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

The **я** forms you already use, out loud:

> люблю́ · не люблю́ · о́чень люблю́ · ненави́жу

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Лю́бишь** and **лю́бите** move the stress to the front, to **лю́-**,
unlike **люблю́**, which stresses the last syllable.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat, noticing the jump:*

> люблю́ — лю́бишь — лю́бит — лю́бите

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Люби́ть follows the говори́ть pattern**, as you already saw with
**смотре́ть**. Today you complete the forms that were missing:

| | люби́ть → любл-/люб- | |
|---|---|---|
| ты | лю́б**ишь** | you like |
| он, она́ | лю́б**ит** | he/she likes |
| вы | лю́б**ите** | you like (formal) |

> — Ты лю́бишь кино́?
> *Do you like the movies?*
> — Да, люблю́. А Ки́ра?
> *Yes, I love them. And Kira?*
> — Она́ лю́бит бале́т.
> *She likes ballet.*
> — А вы, Ве́ра Ива́новна? Вы лю́бите джаз?
> *And you, Vera Ivánovna? Do you like jazz?*
> — Нет, не люблю́. Я люблю́ футбо́л!
> *No, I don't. I love football!*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English swaps "I like" for "you like," "she likes"… with a
different pronoun each time. Russian changes the verb's ENDING, and
the pronoun that goes with it follows the same pattern you already
know from рабо́тать.**

| English (pronoun changes) | Russian (ending changes) |
|---|---|
| I like it / you like it / she likes it | я люблю́ / ты лю́бишь / она́ лю́бит |

It's the same logic you already learned with any other verb: the
subject decides the form, not a separate pronoun stuck to the verb.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read the dialogue from "New" aloud, all four voices.*

### Б. Fill in the correct form.

*Choose the missing form.*

1. Ты ______ кино́? (to like, you)
2. Она́ ______ бале́т. (to like, she)
3. Вы ______ джаз? (to like, formal)
4. Он ______ футбо́л. (to like, he)

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Ты лю́бишь му́зыку?
2. Вы лю́бите фильм?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your survey.** Ask three imaginary people —one with **ты**, one with
**вы**, and report a third one's answer with **он/она́**— whether they
like football, the movies and jazz.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Ты лю́бишь кино́? · Она́ лю́бит бале́т. · Вы лю́бите джаз?
>
> **Now I know:** all six complete forms of **люби́ть**, following the same pattern as **говори́ть**.
