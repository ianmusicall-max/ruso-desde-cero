> **Today** you learn to say what you like and don't like, with a new verb. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **Я люблю́…** and **Я не люблю́…**;
> - name three more hobbies: джаз, рок and бале́т;
> - use them with no change in form, since today you start with the easy case.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Three hobbies you already know, out loud:

> футбо́л — кино́ — ко́фе

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Люблю́** stresses the last syllable, and the **о** in **люблю́** isn't
the same sound as **у**: don't trust how alike they look written down.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> люблю́ — не люблю́
> джаз — рок — бале́т

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Люби́ть** means "to love, to really like": **Я люблю́ футбо́л** is "I
love football," stronger than a plain "I like."

| Word | It sounds like | It means |
|---|---|---|
| люблю́ | lyu-**BLYU** | (I) love, really like |
| не люблю́ | ni lyu-**BLYU** | I don't like |
| джаз | dzhaz | jazz |
| рок | rok | rock |
| бале́т | ba-**LYET** | ballet |

**Today the object doesn't change shape.** Футбо́л, кино́, джаз, рок and
бале́т are all masculine or neuter, and with them **Я люблю́** doesn't
move a single letter:

> — Ты лю́бишь футбо́л? *(you'll get to this later; for now just answer)*
> — Я люблю́ футбо́л. А ты?
> *I love football. And you?*
> — Я не люблю́ футбо́л. Я люблю́ джаз.
> *I don't like football. I love jazz.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English makes the liked thing the object of "like"; Russian makes "I"
the subject and the thing comes after, with no change yet.**

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| I like football. (football is the object) | Я люблю́ футбо́л. (I is the subject) |

Don't look for a separate word for "it" in the Russian sentence: there
isn't one. **Я** is the one feeling it, and **футбо́л** is simply what
is loved —today with no change of form at all.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я люблю́ футбо́л. · Я люблю́ джаз. · Я не люблю́ рок.

### Б. Люблю́ и́ли не люблю́?

*Translate into Russian.*

1. I love the movies.
2. I don't like ballet.
3. I love coffee.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Я люблю́ бале́т.
2. Я не люблю́ джаз.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Five hobbies.** Say out loud, with **люблю́** or **не люблю́**, how you
feel about футбо́л, кино́, ко́фе, джаз and рок.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Я люблю́ футбо́л. · Я не люблю́ джаз. · Я люблю́ бале́т.
>
> **Now I know:** how to use **люблю́/не люблю́**, and that a masculine or neuter object changes nothing.

Tomorrow: the same thing, but with a feminine object.
