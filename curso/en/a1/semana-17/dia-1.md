> **Today there are no new words: there's a review.** You open the free-time unit with two verbs you already conjugate in full. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **гуля́ть** and **отдыха́ть** in all six persons, without hesitating;
> - understand that this unit is about free time;
> - get ready for five new verbs coming this week.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Unit 5's five verbs, from memory:

> де́лать — чита́ть — слу́шать — отдыха́ть — гуля́ть

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Гуля́ю** and **отдыха́ю** stress the same place they always do: the
same spot as the infinitive, like the whole рабо́тать family.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> гуля́ю — гуля́ешь — гуля́ет
> отдыха́ю — отдыха́ешь — отдыха́ет

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**No new grammar: you've known гуля́ть and отдыха́ть since unit 5.**
Today you use them in a conversation about the weekend, which is the
theme of the whole unit.

> — Что ты де́лаешь в выходно́й?
> *What do you do on your day off?*
> — Я гуля́ю. А ты?
> *I go for a walk. And you?*
> — Я отдыха́ю. В выходно́й мы не рабо́таем.
> *I rest. On the day off we don't work.*

**Выходно́й** means "day off," and you already know it from the unit's
episode: today it joins the vocabulary formally.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English doesn't need to review a verb to start a new topic; neither
does Russian, but here it's worth doing on purpose.** This unit is
going to bring five verbs with irregular conjugations, and starting
with two you already master lets you see, by contrast, what's normal
and what's the exception:

| Normal (you already know it) | What's coming this week |
|---|---|
| гуля́ю, гуля́ешь… | писа́ть → **пишу́**, not "писа́ю" |

Keep this contrast in mind: it'll come in handy every day this week.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я гуля́ю. Ты отдыха́ешь? Мы не рабо́таем.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. I go for a walk.
2. Are you resting?
3. We don't work.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. В выходно́й я отдыха́ю.
2. Что ты де́лаешь в выходно́й?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your day off.** Say out loud what you do on a day off, with
**гуля́ю** and **отдыха́ю**, and add a third activity you already know.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** гуля́ть and отдыха́ть in full, and that this unit is about free time.

Tomorrow: playing a sport.
