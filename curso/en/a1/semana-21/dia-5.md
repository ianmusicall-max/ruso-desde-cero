> **Today** you learn the verb "to live," with a form that can't be guessed from the infinitive. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **живу́**, **живёшь**, and **живёт**;
> - combine them with yesterday's countries;
> - say where you really live.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's chunk, from memory:

> в Ита́лии · в Испа́нии · во Фра́нции

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Живу́** is stressed at the end; **живёшь** and **живёт** are
stressed on the **ё**, which is always stressed.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> живу́ — живёшь — живёт

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**The infinitive жить doesn't let you guess the forms: learn them
together, as a chunk.** There's no `-ова-` and no л to explain the
change; it's irregular, like пе́ть in unit 9.

| | жить → жив- |
|---|---|
| я | жив**у́** |
| ты | жив**ёшь** |
| он, она́ | жив**ёт** |

> — Где ты живёшь?
> *Where do you live?*
> — Я живу́ в Росси́и. А Ками́ла живёт в Испа́нии.
> *I live in Russia. And Camila lives in Spain.*
> — А Ми́ша?
> *And Misha?*
> — Он живёт во Фра́нции.
> *He lives in France.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also has "to live" with a fairly stable root ("live,"
"lives"); Russian has the same verb but with a sharper root change:
from `жить` to `жив-`.**

| English (almost fixed root) | Russian (the root changes) |
|---|---|
| I live, you live, he lives | жить → жив**у́**, жив**ёшь**, жив**ёт** |

Just like you already did with пе́ть/пою́, don't look for a rule:
learn the three forms together and repeat them until they come out
on their own.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я живу́ в Росси́и. Ты живёшь в Испа́нии? Он живёт во Фра́нции.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. I live in Italy.
2. Where do you live?
3. She lives in France.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Где живёт Ками́ла?
2. Мы живём в Росси́и.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Where you live.** Say out loud where you really live, and then
say where each course character lives (imaginarily).

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Я живу́ в Росси́и. · Ты живёшь в Испа́нии? · Он живёт во Фра́нции.
>
> **Now I know:** жить is irregular; живу́/живёшь/живёт are learned together, like пе́ть/пою́.
