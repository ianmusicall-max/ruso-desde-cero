> **Today there are no new words: there's a longer text.** You put together everything from the unit into one guided tour of your home. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - chain several sentences without repeating the verb each time;
> - describe a whole room: what's there and where it is;
> - give your own tour, out loud.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's combination, from memory:

> гото́влю на ку́хне · чита́ет в гости́ной

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

Nothing new to read today: review в linked to the next word in
**Вот моя́ кварти́ра** and in **на столе́**.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> Вот моя́ кварти́ра. Э́то моя́ ку́хня.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**A tour is made of three steps, repeated: Вот/Э́то to name
something, what's there, and where it is.** Chaining those three
steps several times, the text grows without you having to learn
anything more:

> Вот моя́ кварти́ра. Э́то моя́ ку́хня. На столе́ телефо́н.
> *Here's my apartment. This is my kitchen. There's a phone on the table.*
> Вот моя́ спа́льня. Су́мка в шкафу́. Ключи́ на по́лке.
> *Here's my bedroom. The bag is in the cupboard. The keys are on the shelf.*

**Notice that "На столе́ телефо́н" needs no verb**: Russian uses no
"there is" or "is" when the word order already makes it clear —the
place first, the object after.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English almost always needs "there is" or has to repeat "is";
Russian, in a place + object sentence, can leave the verb out
entirely.**

| English (needs a verb) | Russian (can skip it) |
|---|---|
| ON the table THERE IS a phone. | На столе́ телефо́н. |

Leaving it out isn't a mistake: it's the most natural way to say it
when the order —place, object— already does the verb's job.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud, chaining the sentences with no long pauses.*

> Вот моя́ кварти́ра. Э́то моя́ ку́хня. На столе́ телефо́н. Вот моя́
> спа́льня. Су́мка в шкафу́.

### Б. Переведи́те.

*Translate into Russian.*

1. Here's my apartment.
2. This is my kitchen.
3. There's a phone on the table.

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Вот моя́ спа́льня. Ключи́ на по́лке.
2. Э́то моя́ гости́ная. Мой люби́мый дива́н здесь.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your own tour.** Describe three rooms of your home out loud, with
today's pattern: name it, say what's there, add where it is.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** how to chain Вот/Э́то, an object, and a place without repeating the verb, to describe a whole home, room by room.
