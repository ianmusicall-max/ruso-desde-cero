> **Today** you learn to answer with a single word, and to show with it how sure you are: **Коне́чно!** · **Наве́рное.** · **Не зна́ю.** By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - answer without repeating the question, the way people really do;
> - grade how sure you are, from **коне́чно** to **не зна́ю**;
> - say **Пра́вда?** with your voice jumping in the right place.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Read these words from yesterday with the stress where it belongs, the
Russian place and not the English one:

> авто́бус — телеви́зор — трамва́й — аэропо́рт — сала́т

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**The question rises; the answer falls.** You've known it since week 1: in a
yes/no question, the voice jumps up on the stressed syllable and drops
(ИК-3); in the answer, it falls (ИК-1). With short answers you hear it better
than ever, because the same word does both jobs:

> Пра́вда? — Пра́вда.
> *Really? — Really.*
> То́чно? — То́чно.
> *Sure? — Sure.*

In **Пра́вда?** the voice jumps on **пра** and drops on the rest. Don't push
it up at the end, on **вда**: that sounds like something else.

**An oddity for tomorrow.** Look at these two words: both have **чн**, and
they don't sound the same.

> то́чно [TOCH-na] — коне́чно [ka-NYESH-na]

In **коне́чно** the ч sounds [sh]. Tomorrow you'll find out why, and which
other words do it.

## 3. New (8–18 min)

Seven short answers:

| Word | Say it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| коне́чно | ka-**NYESH**-na | of course |
| то́чно | **TOCH**-na | exactly, for sure |
| наве́рное | na-**VYER**-na-ye | probably |
| мо́жет быть | **MO**-zhyt byt' | maybe |
| не зна́ю | ni **ZNA**-yoo | I don't know |
| пра́вда | **PRAV**-da | true; really? |
| ла́дно | **LAD**-na | OK, all right |

In **мо́жет** the ж is hard, as always, so the unstressed е sounds like ы:
[**MO**-zhyt]. And learn **не зна́ю** whole: it's "I don't know", and the
verb will come later.

From most to least sure:

> коне́чно · то́чно → наве́рное → мо́жет быть → не зна́ю

> — Э́то Анто́н?
> *Is that Anton?*
> — Наве́рное.
> *Probably.*
> — Пра́вда?
> *Really?*
> — Не зна́ю… Мо́жет быть.
> *I don't know… Maybe.*

**Ла́дно** means accepting: "OK", "all right". Sometimes gladly, sometimes
not: the tone tells you which.

> — Ко́фе?
> *Coffee?*
> — Ла́дно.
> *OK.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**"Really?" rises somewhere else.** In English your voice climbs at the end:
"rea**LLY**?". In Russian it jumps on the stressed syllable and falls after
it: **Пра́**вда? Push it up on **вда** and it sounds strange.

**Наве́рное promises less than it sounds.** It's "probably", not "surely". To
say you're sure, use **коне́чно** or **то́чно**:

| You mean | ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|---|
| Of course it's Anton. | ~~Наве́рное, э́то Анто́н.~~ | Коне́чно, э́то Анто́н. |
| Probably. | ~~Коне́чно.~~ | Наве́рное. |

**And коне́чно isn't pushy.** An English "Of course!" can sound like
"obviously"; Russian **коне́чно** is simply a warm, ready yes.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read each pair: the question first, rising; then the answer, falling.*

> Пра́вда? — Пра́вда.
> То́чно? — То́чно.
> Ла́дно? — Ла́дно.

### Б. Соедини́те.

*Match each answer with its translation.*

1. коне́чно
2. не зна́ю
3. мо́жет быть
4. ла́дно
5. наве́рное
6. пра́вда?

а) OK · б) really? · в) of course · г) probably · д) maybe · е) I don't know

### В. Как отве́тить?

*Answer with a short answer. In brackets, what you think.*

1. — Чай? (you love tea)
2. — Э́то аэропо́рт? (you think so, but not entirely)
3. — Там интерне́т? (you have no idea)
4. — Ко́фе? (you accept, though you'd rather have tea)
5. — Э́то пра́вда? (could be, could not be)

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**The interrogation.** Answer these six questions about your home aloud,
with short answers only, and record yourself:

> Там телеви́зор? · Там компью́тер? · Там интерне́т? · Чай? · Ко́фе? · Э́то пра́вда?

Listen for two things: do your answers fall at the end? Do you say
**коне́чно** with [sh], [ka-NYESH-na]?

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **I can now say:**
> Коне́чно! · То́чно. · Наве́рное. · Мо́жет быть. · Не зна́ю. · Пра́вда? · Ла́дно.
>
> **I know:** **наве́рное** is only "probably"; and **Пра́вда?** jumps on **пра**.

Tomorrow: the words that are written one way and said another.
