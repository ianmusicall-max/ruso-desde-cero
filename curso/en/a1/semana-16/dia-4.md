> **Today** you review a rule from unit 4 and add one new word. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - recognize three consonants you don't hear, from before;
> - use **по́здно**, where the **д** isn't heard either;
> - read any of the four without stumbling.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Unit 4's three words, out loud:

> здра́вствуйте — со́лнце — пра́здник

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**You already know that здра́вствуйте doesn't sound its first в,
со́лнце doesn't sound its л, and пра́здник doesn't sound its д.** Today
a fourth word joins them: **по́здно**, where the **д** isn't heard
either —the same case as пра́здник.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> здра́вствуйте — со́лнце — пра́здник — по́здно

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**По́здно** means "late," and it's the only new word today.

| Word | It sounds like | It means |
|---|---|---|
| по́здно | **POZ**-na | late |

The **д** in **по́здно** drops out of the **-здн-** cluster, just like
in **пра́здник**: learn it alongside that word, not on its own.

> — Что ты де́лаешь? Уже́ по́здно!
> *What are you doing? It's already late!*
> — Я смотрю́ фильм. Сего́дня пра́здник, не рабо́таю.
> *I'm watching a movie. Today's a holiday, I'm not working.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English pronounces every consonant it writes; Russian, in this
specific group of words, doesn't.** The trap is assuming that if it's
written, it's heard:

| It's written | It's heard |
|---|---|
| по́**з**дно | [POZ-na], no **д** |
| пра́**з**дник | [PRAZ-nik], no **д** |

Today's four words are a closed list, not a general rule: not every
**-здн-** or **-лн-** in Russian loses its consonant.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read the four words aloud, slowly the first time.*

> здра́вствуйте · со́лнце · пра́здник · по́здно

### Б. Which consonant isn't heard?

*Choose the silent letter.*

1. со́лнце → а) с · б) л · в) н
2. по́здно → а) п · б) з · в) д
3. здра́вствуйте → а) з · б) в (the first one) · в) т
4. пра́здник → а) п · б) р · в) д

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Уже́ по́здно.
2. Сего́дня пра́здник.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Record yourself.** Read the four words aloud and listen back: does
the silent consonant drop in all four?

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I know:** the four words with a written consonant that isn't heard —здра́вствуйте, со́лнце, пра́здник and, since today, по́здно.
