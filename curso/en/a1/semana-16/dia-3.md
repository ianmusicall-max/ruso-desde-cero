> **Today** you add a fourth degree, stronger than yesterday's three. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - use **ненави́жу**, "I hate," as a fixed form for **я**;
> - tell when to say **не люблю́** and when to say **ненави́жу**;
> - keep the direct object the same as always.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's three degrees, out loud:

> о́чень люблю́ · люблю́ · не люблю́

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

**Ненави́жу** stresses the third syllable, **-ви́-**, and the **ж**
sounds like the French "j," not like an English one.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> не люблю́ — ненави́жу

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Ненави́жу** is much stronger than **не люблю́**: it means "I hate,"
not just "I don't like." Today you learn it only as a fixed form for
**я**; conjugating it fully comes later.

| Word | It sounds like | It means |
|---|---|---|
| ненави́жу | ni-na-**VI**-zhu | I hate |

The direct object stays the same as in yesterday's three degrees:

> — Ты лю́бишь рок?
> *Do you like rock?*
> — Нет! Я не люблю́ рок. Я ненави́жу футбо́л.
> *No! I don't like rock. I hate football.*
> — Ой, а я люблю́ футбо́л.
> *Oh, well I love football.*

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English also has "hate" separate from "don't like"; the difference
is one of degree, not structure, in both languages.**

| English | Russian |
|---|---|
| I don't like rock | не люблю́ рок |
| I hate football | ненави́жу футбо́л |

The trap isn't grammar: it's using **ненави́жу** for something that
just mildly annoys you. Save it for what you truly can't stand.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Не люблю́ рок. · Ненави́жу футбо́л. · Люблю́ джаз.

### Б. Which one is stronger?

*Choose the stronger of the two.*

1. не люблю́ / ненави́жу
2. люблю́ / о́чень люблю́

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Я ненави́жу рок.
2. Я ненави́жу бале́т.

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Your full scale.** Pick four hobbies from the unit and say each one
with a different degree: о́чень люблю́, люблю́, не люблю́, ненави́жу.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Не люблю́ рок. · Ненави́жу футбо́л.
>
> **Now I know:** all four full degrees, from strongest to weakest, with the same direct object throughout.
