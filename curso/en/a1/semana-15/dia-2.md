> **Today** you find out that a feminine object does change after **люблю́**. By the end you'll be able to:
>
> - swap **-а** for **-у** and **-я** for **-ю** in the direct object;
> - say **Я люблю́ му́зыку** and **Я люблю́ пе́сню**;
> - use **пе́сня**, a new word.

## 1. Warm-up (0–3 min)

Yesterday's, unchanged, out loud:

> Я люблю́ футбо́л. Я люблю́ джаз.

## 2. Pronunciation (3–8 min)

The **у** in **му́зыку** isn't stressed: it's still said clearly, just
without the force of the stressed syllable, which stays on **му́-**.

**Слу́шайте и повторя́йте.**
*Listen and repeat:*

> му́зыка — му́зыку
> пе́сня — пе́сню

## 3. New (8–18 min)

**Yesterday the object didn't change because it was masculine or
neuter. Today it does, because it's feminine.** With a noun ending in
**-а** or **-я**, the ending turns into **-у** or **-ю** when it's the
direct object of a verb like **люби́ть**:

| Presenting it (no change) | As a direct object |
|---|---|
| Э́то му́зыка. | Я люблю́ му́зык**у**. |
| Э́то пе́сня. | Я люблю́ пе́сн**ю**. |

**Пе́сня** means "song," and it's new today.

> — Ты лю́бишь му́зыку?
> *Do you like music?*
> — Да, я люблю́ му́зыку. И я люблю́ э́ту пе́сню.
> *Yes, I love music. And I love this song.*

**Э́ту пе́сню** is "this song" as an object: learn it as a chunk —the
reason behind **э́ту** comes later.

### ⚠️ Contrast with English

**English never touches the noun; Russian does, but only when it's the
direct object.** "Music" is "music" in any English sentence; **му́зыка**
turns into **му́зыку** right after a verb like **люби́ть**:

| English (never changes) | Russian (changes as an object) |
|---|---|
| I like music. / It's music. | Я люблю́ му́зыку. / Э́то му́зыка. |

The clue is simple: if the noun comes AFTER **люблю́**, it changes; if
you're just presenting it with **Э́то**, it stays the same.

## 4. Practice (18–25 min)

### А. Чита́йте.

*Read aloud.*

> Я люблю́ му́зыку. · Я люблю́ пе́сню. · Я люблю́ пи́ццу.

### Б. -а or -у, -я or -ю?

*Choose the correct form.*

1. Я люблю́ ______ (му́зыка).
2. Я люблю́ ______ (пе́сня).
3. Э́то ______ (му́зыка).
4. Я люблю́ ______ (пи́цца).

### В. Переведи́те.

*Translate into English.*

1. Я люблю́ э́ту пе́сню.
2. Ты лю́бишь пи́ццу?

## 5. Your turn (25–29 min)

**Three feminine things.** Say **Я люблю́…** out loud with му́зыка,
пе́сня and пи́цца, changing the ending each time.

## 6. Wrap-up (29–30 min)

> **Now I can say:**
> Я люблю́ му́зыку. · Я люблю́ пе́сню. · Я люблю́ пи́ццу.
>
> **Now I know:** a feminine object changes **-а** → **-у** and **-я** → **-ю** after **люблю́**.
